朗斯基行列式与阿贝尔公式The Wronskian and Abel’s formula

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

两个解看起来不同，也可能只是常数倍。把它们及其导数放进一个行列式，可以在适用条件下检验独立性。

Two expressions may differ yet be constant multiples. The Wronskian combines solutions and derivatives to test independence under the relevant equation assumptions.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

二阶齐次线性方程的解空间在一个正则区间内是二维的。两个解能否作为一组基底，由 W=y1y2′−y1′y2 判断。一个点的 W≠0 已能保证整个正则区间内线性独立。

On a regular interval, the solution space of a second-order homogeneous linear equation is two-dimensional. The Wronskian W=y1y2′−y1′y2 tests whether two solutions form a basis. If W≠0 at one point, they are linearly independent throughout that regular interval.

W(x)=W(x0)exp[−∫x0xP(t)dt]
使用条件When it applies

标准式 y′′+P(x)y′+Q(x)y=0，P,Q 在连通区间连续；两个函数必须是该方程的解。

Use the standard form y′′+P(x)y′+Q(x)y=0, where P,Q are continuous on a connected interval. Both functions must solve this same equation.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

由定义求导：W′=y1y2′′−y1′′y2。代入 yi′′=−Pyi′−Qyi，Q 项相消，得到 W′=−PW。分离变量，或用一阶方程积分因子，得到阿贝尔公式。若 W(x0)=0，初值向量相关，再用线性初值问题唯一性推出两个解线性相关。

Differentiate the definition: W′=y1y2′′−y1′′y2. Substitute yi′′=−Pyi′−Qyi. The Q terms cancel, leaving W′=−PW. Separating variables, or using an integrating factor for this first-order equation, gives Abel’s formula. If W(x0)=0, the initial-data vectors are dependent. Uniqueness of the linear initial-value problem then makes the two solutions linearly dependent.

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

判断 ex,e−x 是否可组成 y′′−y=0 的基本解组。

Can ex,e−x form a fundamental solution set for y′′−y=0?

解答

Solution

W=ex(−e−x)−exe−x=−2≠0，所以是基本解组；通解为 C1ex+C2e−x。

W=ex(−e−x)−exe−x=−2≠0, so they do form a fundamental set. The general solution is C1ex+C2e−x.

记忆线索Memory cue

先除最高阶系数，再读出一阶导系数；W 的指数中带负号。

First divide by the leading coefficient. Read off the coefficient of the first derivative; its integral enters the exponent with a minus sign.

常点幂级数与正则奇点 Frobenius 法Power series at ordinary points and Frobenius series at regular singular points

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

遇到没有熟悉公式的微分方程，可以先假设解是一串幂，再比较每次幂的系数。先判断展开点是否普通；奇点可能需要额外的起始幂。

When no familiar formula solves an ODE, assume a power series and compare coefficients. Classify the expansion point first; a regular singular point may require a leading power.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

常点用 y=∑an(x−x0)n；正则奇点用 y=(x−x0)r∑an(x−x0)n。最低次幂给指标方程，其余各次幂给递推。特殊函数的多项式解来自递推系数在某一级变成零。

At an ordinary point, use y=∑an(x−x0)n. At a regular singular point, use y=(x−x0)r∑an(x−x0)n. The lowest power gives the indicial equation; the remaining powers give a recurrence. A special-function solution becomes a polynomial when the recurrence terminates.

y=(x−x0)r∑n=0∞an(x−x0)n,a0≠0
使用条件When it applies

先写标准式 y′′+Py′+Qy=0。若 (x−x0)P、(x−x0)2Q 解析，则为正则奇点。整数差或重指标根需检查对数解。

First write y′′+Py′+Qy=0. The point is regular singular if (x−x0)P and (x−x0)2Q are analytic there. Repeated indicial roots or roots differing by an integer require a check for logarithmic solutions.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

若 (x−x0)P=p0+p1(x−x0)+⋯，(x−x0)2Q=q0+q1(x−x0)+⋯，代入后最低次幂系数是 a0[r(r−1)+p0r+q0]，故指标方程为 r(r−1)+p0r+q0=0。之后逐次比较系数，得到 an 递推；不可只求指标根就停止。

Write (x−x0)P=p0+p1(x−x0)+⋯ and (x−x0)2Q=q0+q1(x−x0)+⋯. After substitution, the coefficient of the lowest power is a0[r(r−1)+p0r+q0]. Thus the indicial equation is r(r−1)+p0r+q0=0. Comparing each subsequent power gives the recurrence for an. Finding only the indicial roots does not finish the solution.

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

求 x2y′′+xy′−ν2y=0 的指标根及解。

Find the indicial roots and solutions of x2y′′+xy′−ν2y=0.

解答

Solution

代入 y=xr 得 r(r−1)+r−ν2=r2−ν2=0。ν≠0 时 y=C1xν+C2x−ν（取 x>0）；ν=0 时第二解为 lnx。

Substituting y=xr gives r(r−1)+r−ν2=r2−ν2=0. For ν≠0, y=C1xν+C2x−ν (take x>0). For ν=0, the second solution is lnx.

记忆线索Memory cue

最低幂找指数，同幂比较找递推，分母为零查对数。

Use the lowest power to find exponents, compare equal powers to find the recurrence, and check logarithms when a denominator vanishes.

二阶非齐次常微分方程Second-order nonhomogeneous ODEs

本节无书后习题No end-of-section exercises
知识点 Concept 理解后再记忆Understand before memorizing

常数变易法Variation of parameters

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

已经知道齐次方程的两个解，就把其中的常数改成待定函数，用它们吸收外力项。先把最高阶导数系数化为1。

Start with two homogeneous solutions and replace their constants by functions to absorb the forcing. Normalize the highest-derivative coefficient before applying the formulas.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

非齐次线性方程的通解等于齐次通解加一个特解。让齐次解前的常数变为函数，并增加一个辅助条件，可把求特解降为两个积分。

The general solution of a nonhomogeneous linear equation is the homogeneous general solution plus one particular solution. Replace the constants multiplying homogeneous solutions by functions. An extra auxiliary condition reduces the task to two integrations.

yp=−y1∫y2fWdx+y2∫y1fWdx
使用条件When it applies

针对标准式 y′′+Py′+Qy=f，y1,y2 为齐次基本解，W=y1y2′−y1′y2≠0。非标准式需先除最高阶系数。

For y′′+Py′+Qy=f, let y1,y2 be a homogeneous fundamental set with W=y1y2′−y1′y2≠0. If the equation is not in standard form, divide by the leading coefficient first.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

令 yp=u1y1+u2y2，规定 u1′y1+u2′y2=0。代入方程后剩 u1′y1′+u2′y2′=f。解二元方程得 u1′=−y2f/W、u2′=y1f/W，分别积分即得公式。积分常数已经包含在齐次通解中。

Set yp=u1y1+u2y2 and impose u1′y1+u2′y2=0. Substitution leaves u1′y1′+u2′y2′=f. Solving these two equations gives u1′=−y2f/W and u2′=y1f/W. Integrate to obtain the displayed formula. Any integration constants are already included in the homogeneous general solution.

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

求 y′′+y=1 的通解。

Find the general solution of y′′+y=1.

解答

Solution

取 y1=cosx,y2=sinx,W=1。yp=−cosx∫sinxdx+sinx∫cosxdx=cos2x+sin2x=1，故 y=C1cosx+C2sinx+1。

Take y1=cosx,y2=sinx,W=1. Then yp=−cosx∫sinxdx+sinx∫cosxdx=cos2x+sin2x=1. Hence y=C1cosx+C2sinx+1.

记忆线索Memory cue

先齐次，后特解；变易积分前检查标准式。

Solve the homogeneous equation first, then find a particular solution. Check standard form before integrating.

利用朗斯基行列式证明函数组 {xn/n!, n=0,1,…,N} 线性独立。

Use the Wronskian to prove that {xn/n!, n=0,1,…,N} is linearly independent.

这道题要你做什么What are you trying to find?

证明这些函数没有多余的一项：任何一项都不能由其余项凑出。

Prove that none of these functions is redundant: no one is a combination of the others.

看到什么，就想到这个方法Clue → choice of method

看到“函数组线性独立”，先算 W。W 是把函数及其各阶导数排成方阵后的行列式。

For linear independence, calculate W: the determinant whose rows contain successive derivatives.

    Write the first functions: 1, x, x²/2, ordered by increasing degree.

  2. 第一行放函数，第二行放一阶导数，依次写到 N 阶导数。

    A derivative beyond the polynomial degree is zero. Every diagonal entry is 1, so W=1.

  4. W 不为 0，齐次线性方程组只有零系数解，因此函数组独立。

    A nonzero determinant forces all coefficients in a zero linear combination to vanish.

W 恒为零时何时能推出相关When does an identically zero Wronskian imply dependence?

如果两个函数 y1(x) 和 y2(x) 的朗斯基行列式恒为零，证明它们满足 y1(x)=cy2(x)，c 为常数，即线性相关。

If the Wronskian of two functions y1(x) and y2(x) is identically zero, prove that y1(x)=cy2(x) for a constant c, so they are linearly dependent.

读题注意Read this first

对任意可微函数，W≡0 未必推出整个区间内线性相关；需要同一正规齐次方程解等额外条件。解析给出反例与正确证明。

For arbitrary differentiable functions, W≡0 need not imply linear dependence on the whole interval. An extra assumption, such as solving the same regular homogeneous equation, is needed. The solution gives both a counterexample and the corrected proof.

这道题要你做什么What are you trying to find?

分清“W=0”能证明到哪里，避免证明一个缺条件的命题。

Identify exactly what W=0 proves, rather than prove a statement with missing assumptions.

看到什么，就想到这个方法Clue → choice of method

W=0 适合对 y₁/y₂ 求导，但只有 y₂ 不为 0 时才能这样除。

W=0 suggests differentiating y₁/y₂, but division requires y₂ to be nonzero.

不会做？带我一步一步做Guide me, one step at a time＋

    Work first on a connected interval where y₂ does not vanish and differentiate the quotient.

  2. 把导数分子认成 −W；W=0 就说明比值是常数。

    Recognize the numerator as −W. If W=0, the quotient is constant.

  3. 若要跨过 y₂ 的零点，补充两函数是同一正规齐次方程的解，再用初值唯一性。

    To cross zeros of y₂, assume both solve the same regular homogeneous equation and use uniqueness of initial data.

  4. 检查题干若只说任意光滑函数，就说明反例：左右两侧各放一个光滑凸起，W=0 却不成比例。

    If the question allows arbitrary smooth functions, give the counterexample with separate smooth bumps on the two sides.

No. A nonzero y₁ cannot equal the zero right side, although the pair is still linearly dependent.

阿贝尔公式及教材公式的条件Abel’s formula and the condition missing from the textbook formula

证明二阶线性齐次常微分方程 p(x)y′′+q(x)y′+r(x)y=0 的朗斯基行列式为 L=C/p(x)，其中 C 为常数。

Prove that the Wronskian of p(x)y′′+q(x)y′+r(x)y=0 is L=C/p(x), where C is constant.

读题注意Read this first

原命题缺少 q=p′ 条件。一般 Abel 公式为 W=Cexp(−∫q/pdx)；只有自伴形式才有 W=C/p。

The original statement lacks the condition q=p′. The general Abel formula is W=Cexp(−∫q/pdx). Only the self-adjoint form gives W=C/p.

这道题要你做什么What are you trying to find?

从原方程推出正确的 W 公式，并找出 C/p 所需条件。

Derive the correct formula for W and find the assumption needed for C/p.

看到什么，就想到这个方法Clue → choice of method

题目给了最高阶系数 p，先除以 p；真正进入 W 公式的是一阶导系数 q/p。

Divide by the leading coefficient p first. The coefficient used in Abel’s formula is q/p.

不会做？带我一步一步做Guide me, one step at a time＋

  1. 在 p 不为 0 的区间，把 y″ 写成 −(q/p)y′−(r/p)y。
