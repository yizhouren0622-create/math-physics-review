二阶齐次常微分方程解的线性关系Linear relations among solutions of second-order homogeneous ODEs

练习这一节 →Practice this section →
知识点 Concept 理解后再记忆Understand before memorizing

朗斯基行列式与阿贝尔公式The Wronskian and Abel’s formula

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

两个解看起来不同，也可能只是常数倍。把它们及其导数放进一个行列式，可以在适用条件下检验独立性。

Two expressions may differ yet be constant multiples. The Wronskian combines solutions and derivatives to test independence under the relevant equation assumptions.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

二阶齐次线性方程的解空间在一个正则区间内是二维的。两个解能否作为一组基底，由 W=y1y2′−y1′y2 判断。一个点的 W≠0 已能保证整个正则区间内线性独立。

On a regular interval, the solution space of a second-order homogeneous linear equation is two-dimensional. The Wronskian W=y1y2′−y1′y2 tests whether two solutions form a basis. If W≠0 at one point, they are linearly independent throughout that regular interval.

W(x)=W(x0)exp[−∫x0xP(t)dt]
使用条件When it applies

标准式 y′′+P(x)y′+Q(x)y=0，P,Q 在连通区间连续；两个函数必须是该方程的解。

Use the standard form y′′+P(x)y′+Q(x)y=0, where P,Q are continuous on a connected interval. Both functions must solve this same equation.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

先把方程除以最高阶系数，写成 y″+Py′+Qy=0。定义 W=y1y2′−y1′y2，求导时两个 y1′y2′ 项相消，故 W′=y1y2′′−y1′′y2。

Divide by the leading coefficient to write y″+Py′+Qy=0. Define W=y1y2′−y1′y2; differentiating cancels the two y1′y2′ terms, leaving W′=y1y2′′−y1′′y2.

对两个解使用 yi′′=−Pyi′−Qyi，代入后含 Q 的项相消，得到 W′=−PW。乘积分因子 e∫x0xP(t)dt，其乘积导数为零，所以 W(x)=W(x0)e−∫x0xP(t)dt；这种写法也允许 W(x0)=0。

Substitute yi′′=−Pyi′−Qyi for both solutions. The Q terms cancel and give W′=−PW. Multiplication by integrating factor e∫x0xP(t)dt gives a constant product, so W(x)=W(x0)e−∫x0xP(t)dt, including the case W(x0)=0.

指数因子从不为零，因此 W 要么整个正则区间非零，要么恒为零。前者意味着两个初值向量独立，可唯一配出任意初值；后者意味着初值向量相关，由线性方程初值唯一性推出两个解相关。

The exponential never vanishes, so W is either nonzero throughout the regular interval or identically zero. A nonzero value makes the initial-data vectors independent, allowing arbitrary data to be matched; a zero value makes them dependent, and initial-value uniqueness extends that dependence to the solutions.

以上结论要求两个函数是同一正则齐次方程的解。对任意光滑函数，W≡0 未必推出全局线性相关；若原式为 py″+qy′+ry=0，一般指数里是 q/p，只有 q=p′ 时才化为 W=C/p。

These conclusions require two solutions of the same regular homogeneous equation. Arbitrary smooth functions can have W≡0 without global dependence. For py″+qy′+ry=0, the general exponent uses q/p; it reduces to W=C/p only when q=p′.

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

判断 ex,e−x 是否可组成 y′′−y=0 的基本解组。

Can ex,e−x form a fundamental solution set for y′′−y=0?

解答

Solution

先求导 y1′=ex、y2′=−e−x，代入得 W=ex(−e−x)−exe−x=−1−1=−2。因为非零，两函数独立。

Differentiate to get y1′=ex and y2′=−e−x, then W=ex(−e−x)−exe−x=−1−1=−2. Its nonzero value proves independence.

再检查 yi′′−yi=0，所以两者确实是该方程的解，而不只是任意独立函数。故基本解组成立，通解为 C1ex+C2e−x；本题 P=0，阿贝尔公式也预言 W 为常数。

Check yi′′−yi=0, confirming both solve this equation rather than merely being independent functions. They form a fundamental set, giving C1ex+C2e−x. Here P=0, so Abel's formula also predicts constant W.

在实轴上，验证 y1=e2x、y2=xe2x 是否为 y″−4y′+4y=0 的基本解组。计算朗斯基行列式，并求满足 y(0)=1、y′(0)=3 的解。

On the real line, verify whether y1=e2x and y2=xe2x form a fundamental set for y″−4y′+4y=0. Calculate the Wronskian and find the solution satisfying y(0)=1 and y′(0)=3.

提示 1：先选方法Hint 1: choose a method＋

先确认两个函数确实满足同一个齐次方程，再求 W=y1y2′−y1′y2。

First verify that both functions solve the same homogeneous equation, then calculate W=y1y2′−y1′y2.

提示 2：写第一行Hint 2: write the first line＋

有 y1′=2e2x、y2′=(1+2x)e2x，因此 W=e4x。

We have y1′=2e2x and y2′=(1+2x)e2x, giving W=e4x.

提示 3：关键计算Hint 3: key calculation＋

写 y=(A+Bx)e2x，初值依次给 A=1、2A+B=3。

Write y=(A+Bx)e2x; the initial data give A=1 and 2A+B=3.

展开完整解答Open the full solution＋

第一函数有 y1′′=4e2x，直接代入为零。第二函数有 y2′=(1+2x)e2x、y2′′=(4+4x)e2x；代入也得到 y2′′−4y2′+4y2=0。

For the first function, y1′′=4e2x gives zero upon substitution. For the second, y2′=(1+2x)e2x and y2′′=(4+4x)e2x; substitution also gives y2′′−4y2′+4y2=0.

计算 W=e2x(1+2x)e2x−2e2xxe2x=e4x。它处处非零，因此两个解线性独立，组成基本解组。方程标准式的一阶导系数为 P=−4，阿贝尔公式预测 W=W(0)e4x，与计算一致。

Calculate W=e2x(1+2x)e2x−2e2xxe2x=e4x. It is nonzero everywhere, so the two solutions are independent and form a fundamental set. The standard equation has first-derivative coefficient P=−4, so Abel's formula predicts W=W(0)e4x, consistent with the direct calculation.

通解为 y=(A+Bx)e2x。在零点，y(0)=A=1，y′(0)=2A+B=3，所以 B=1。所求解为 y=(1+x)e2x。

The general solution is y=(A+Bx)e2x. At zero, y(0)=A=1 and y′(0)=2A+B=3, so B=1. The required solution is y=(1+x)e2x.

怎样检查，哪里容易错Check your result and common mistakes＋

检查所求解满足方程和两条初值。典型错误是把 xe2x 的导数写成 2xe2x，漏掉乘积法则中的 e2x。

Check that the result satisfies the equation and both initial conditions. A common error is to differentiate xe2x as 2xe2x, omitting the e2x term from the product rule.

二阶齐次常微分方程的级数解法Series solutions of second-order homogeneous ODEs

练习这一节 →Practice this section →
知识点 Concept 理解后再记忆Understand before memorizing

常点幂级数与正则奇点 Frobenius 法Power series at ordinary points and Frobenius series at regular singular points

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

遇到没有熟悉公式的微分方程，可以先假设解是一串幂，再比较每次幂的系数。先判断展开点是否普通；奇点可能需要额外的起始幂。

When no familiar formula solves an ODE, assume a power series and compare coefficients. Classify the expansion point first; a regular singular point may require a leading power.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

常点用 y=∑an(x−x0)n；正则奇点用 y=(x−x0)r∑an(x−x0)n。最低次幂给指标方程，其余各次幂给递推。特殊函数的多项式解来自递推系数在某一级变成零。

At an ordinary point, use y=∑an(x−x0)n. At a regular singular point, use y=(x−x0)r∑an(x−x0)n. The lowest power gives the indicial equation; the remaining powers give a recurrence. A special-function solution becomes a polynomial when the recurrence terminates.

y=(x−x0)r∑n=0∞an(x−x0)n,a0≠0
使用条件When it applies

先写标准式 y′′+Py′+Qy=0。若 (x−x0)P、(x−x0)2Q 解析，则为正则奇点。整数差或重指标根需检查对数解。

First write y′′+Py′+Qy=0. The point is regular singular if (x−x0)P and (x−x0)2Q are analytic there. Repeated indicial roots or roots differing by an integer require a check for logarithmic solutions.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

先移中心，令 t=x−x0，再写标准式 y″+P(t)y′+Q(t)y=0。若 P,Q 在零点解析，用普通幂级数；若 tP=p0+p1t+⋯、t2Q=q0+q1t+⋯ 解析，则采用正则奇点形式 y=tr∑n≥0antn，a0≠0。

Shift the center using t=x−x0 and write y″+P(t)y′+Q(t)y=0. If P,Q are analytic at zero, use an ordinary power series. If tP=p0+p1t+⋯ and t2Q=q0+q1t+⋯ are analytic, use y=tr∑n≥0antn with a0≠0 at a regular singular point.

将方程乘 t2。最低幂 tr 的三个贡献分别为 a0r(r−1)、a0p0r、a0q0；因为 a0≠0，指标方程是 r(r−1)+p0r+q0=0。它只决定允许的起始指数，还没有决定整个解。

Multiply the equation by t2. The three contributions to the lowest power tr are a0r(r−1), a0p0r, and a0q0. Since a0≠0, the indicial equation is r(r−1)+p0r+q0=0. It determines allowed starting exponents, not the whole solution.

继续比较每个 tn+r 的系数，通常可以逐级解出 an。每次都要检查分母；指标差非整数时通常得到两支，重根或整数差时第二支可能含对数，也可能因特殊参数不含对数，不能只凭根差就武断决定。

Continue comparing coefficients of each tn+r to determine an recursively. Check every denominator. Noninteger root differences usually yield two series; repeated roots or integer differences can require a logarithm, although special parameters can avoid it.

多项式来自递推终止：某一级后所有更高系数都为零，而非只看到一个系数偶然为零。正则奇点法不适用于任意不规则奇点；级数的收敛域也受其他奇点位置限制。

A polynomial requires recurrence termination, with all later coefficients zero, not merely one accidental zero. Frobenius series do not cover arbitrary irregular singularities, and nearby singularities constrain convergence.

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

求 x2y′′+xy′−ν2y=0 的指标根及解。

Find the indicial roots and solutions of x2y′′+xy′−ν2y=0.

解答

Solution

取 x>0 并试 y=xr，有 y′=rxr−1、y″=r(r−1)xr−2。代入 x2y″+xy′−ν2y=0，得到 [r(r−1)+r−ν2]xr=(r2−ν2)xr=0，所以 r=±ν。

On x>0, try y=xr, with y′=rxr−1 and y″=r(r−1)xr−2. Substitution into x2y″+xy′−ν2y=0 gives [r(r−1)+r−ν2]xr=(r2−ν2)xr=0, so r=±ν.

若 ν≠0，两支 xν,x−ν 的 W=−2ν/x≠0，故通解为 C1xν+C2x−ν。即使指标差是整数，本例也不自动产生对数，因为两支已经独立。

If ν≠0, the two solutions xν,x−ν have W=−2ν/x≠0, giving C1xν+C2x−ν. Even an integer root difference does not force a logarithm here, since the two powers are already independent.

若 ν=0，只有常数支。原方程可写 (xy′)′=0，积分得 xy′=C2、y=C1+C2lnx；这实际展示了重根的第二支如何产生。

If ν=0, there is only a constant power solution. Rewriting the equation as (xy′)′=0 gives xy′=C2, then y=C1+C2lnx, explicitly showing how the repeated-root second solution arises.

在 x=0 附近用幂级数求 y″+2xy′+2y=0，初值为 y(0)=1、y′(0)=0。先判断展开点是常点还是正则奇点，再给出递推式、前三个非零项以及可识别的闭式解。

Use a power series near x=0 to solve y″+2xy′+2y=0 with y(0)=1 and y′(0)=0. First classify the expansion point as ordinary or regular singular, then give the recurrence, the first three nonzero terms, and a recognizable closed-form solution.

提示 1：先选方法Hint 1: choose a method＋

最高阶系数为一，其余系数解析，因此是常点，用 y=∑n=0∞anxn 即可。

The leading coefficient is one and the other coefficients are analytic, so this is an ordinary point. Use y=∑n=0∞anxn.

提示 2：写第一行Hint 2: write the first line＋

同次幂比较得 (n+2)(n+1)an+2+(2n+2)an=0。

Comparing equal powers gives (n+2)(n+1)an+2+(2n+2)an=0.

提示 3：关键计算Hint 3: key calculation＋

由 a0=1、a1=0，得到 a2k=(−1)k/k!，所有奇次系数为零。

Starting from a0=1 and a1=0, obtain a2k=(−1)k/k!, with all odd coefficients zero.

展开完整解答Open the full solution＋

标准式中 P=2x、Q=2 在原点解析，所以原点是常点。这里不需要额外的指标因子；设 y=∑n=0∞anxn，初值给 a0=1、a1=0。

In standard form, P=2x and Q=2 are analytic at the origin, so it is an ordinary point. No additional indicial factor is needed. Set y=∑n=0∞anxn; the initial data give a0=1 and a1=0.

二阶导数改写为 y″=∑n=0∞(n+2)(n+1)an+2xn；另外 2xy′+2y=∑n=0∞(2n+2)anxn。比较系数得到 an+2=−2an/(n+2)。

Rewrite the second derivative as y″=∑n=0∞(n+2)(n+1)an+2xn. Also, 2xy′+2y=∑n=0∞(2n+2)anxn. Comparing coefficients gives an+2=−2an/(n+2).

从偶次链依次得到 a2=−1、a4=1/2；奇次链由 a1=0 始终为零。故前三个非零项为 y=1−x2+x4/2+⋯，一般偶次系数为 a2k=(−1)k/k!。

The even chain gives a2=−1 and a4=1/2, while the odd chain stays zero because a1=0. Thus the first three nonzero terms are y=1−x2+x4/2+⋯, with general even coefficient a2k=(−1)k/k!.

识别指数级数，得到 y=e−x2。此级数对所有实数收敛；直接代回后确认为原初值问题的解。展开点分类决定的是选用哪种级数形式，不能看到二阶方程就一律先写 Frobenius 指标方程。

Recognizing the exponential series gives y=e−x2. The series converges for every real argument, and direct substitution confirms the initial-value problem. Classifying the expansion point determines the series form; a second-order equation does not automatically require a Frobenius indicial equation.

怎样检查，哪里容易错Check your result and common mistakes＋

计算 y′=−2xe−x2、y″=(4x2−2)e−x2，代入为零。典型错误是给二阶导数移指标时漏掉两个连续因子，或把奇偶两条递推链混在一起。

Calculate y′=−2xe−x2 and y″=(4x2−2)e−x2; substitution gives zero. Common errors are dropping one of the two consecutive factors when shifting the second-derivative index or mixing the even and odd recurrence chains.

二阶非齐次常微分方程Second-order nonhomogeneous ODEs

本节无书后习题No end-of-section exercises
知识点 Concept 理解后再记忆Understand before memorizing

常数变易法Variation of parameters

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

已经知道齐次方程的两个解，就把其中的常数改成待定函数，用它们吸收外力项。先把最高阶导数系数化为1。

Start with two homogeneous solutions and replace their constants by functions to absorb the forcing. Normalize the highest-derivative coefficient before applying the formulas.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

非齐次线性方程的通解等于齐次通解加一个特解。让齐次解前的常数变为函数，并增加一个辅助条件，可把求特解降为两个积分。

The general solution of a nonhomogeneous linear equation is the homogeneous general solution plus one particular solution. Replace the constants multiplying homogeneous solutions by functions. An extra auxiliary condition reduces the task to two integrations.

yp=−y1∫y2fWdx+y2∫y1fWdx
使用条件When it applies

针对标准式 y′′+Py′+Qy=f，y1,y2 为齐次基本解，W=y1y2′−y1′y2≠0。非标准式需先除最高阶系数。

For y′′+Py′+Qy=f, let y1,y2 be a homogeneous fundamental set with W=y1y2′−y1′y2≠0. If the equation is not in standard form, divide by the leading coefficient first.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

先求标准齐次方程的基本解 y1,y2，确保 W=y1y2′−y1′y2≠0。非齐次特解设为 yp=u1y1+u2y2，并规定辅助条件 u1′y1+u2′y2=0，以消去求导中多出的项。

Find a fundamental pair y1,y2 for the standard homogeneous equation and check W=y1y2′−y1′y2≠0. Set yp=u1y1+u2y2 and impose u1′y1+u2′y2=0 to simplify differentiation.

在辅助条件下，yp′=u1y1′+u2y2′，再求导得到 yp′′=u1′y1′+u2′y2′+u1y1′′+u2y2′′。代入 yp′′+Pyp′+Qyp=f，含 ui 的齐次方程项抵消，剩 u1′y1′+u2′y2′=f。

The auxiliary condition gives yp′=u1y1′+u2y2′ and yp′′=u1′y1′+u2′y2′+u1y1′′+u2y2′′. Substituting into yp′′+Pyp′+Qyp=f cancels the homogeneous terms involving ui, leaving u1′y1′+u2′y2′=f.

连同辅助条件，这是关于 u1′,u2′ 的二元一次方程组。用行列式 W 解得 u1′=−y2f/W、u2′=y1f/W；分别积分并代回，得 yp=−y1∫y2f/Wdx+y2∫y1f/Wdx。

Together with the auxiliary condition, this is a two-by-two linear system for u1′,u2′. Its determinant W gives u1′=−y2f/W and u2′=y1f/W. Integrating yields yp=−y1∫y2f/Wdx+y2∫y1f/Wdx.

Add the homogeneous solution at the end; it already includes the integration constants. For ay″+by′+cy=g, first divide by a, so the formula uses f=g/a. Restrict to regular intervals that exclude points with a=0.
