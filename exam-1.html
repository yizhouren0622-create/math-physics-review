第 1 题Question 112 分 · C–R 条件12 points · Cauchy–Riemann conditions

可导，不一定解析Differentiable at a point; analytic on a region

设 f(z)=|z|2。判断其可导点、解析区域，并求所有存在的复导数。

Let f(z)=|z|2. Find every point of complex differentiability, every region of analyticity, and all complex derivatives that exist.

展开提示Open hint＋

写 u=x2+y2,v=0。先解 C–R 条件，再用定义核验候选点。

Write u=x2+y2,v=0. Solve the Cauchy–Riemann conditions first, then check the candidate point directly from the derivative definition.

展开分步答案与评分点Open step-by-step solution and rubric＋

ux=2x,uy=2y,vx=vy=0，C–R 条件只在 (0,0) 成立。

对原点直接计算 [f(h)−f(0)]/h=|h|2/h=h¯→0，故 f′(0)=0。

其他点不满足必要条件所以不可导。

任何开邻域都包含非原点，所以函数无处解析。

We have ux=2x,uy=2y,vx=vy=0.

The Cauchy–Riemann conditions hold only at (0,0).

At the origin, the difference quotient is [f(h)−f(0)]/h=|h|2/h=h¯→0, so f′(0)=0.

Elsewhere the necessary conditions fail, so no complex derivative exists.

Every open neighborhood contains a point other than the origin; consequently the function is nowhere analytic.

第 3 题Question 312 分 · 闭路积分12 points · Closed-contour integral

高阶柯西积分公式Cauchy’s derivative formula

计算 ∮|z|=2ez/(z−1)3dz，曲线逆时针。

Evaluate ∮|z|=2ez/(z−1)3dz, with counterclockwise orientation.

展开提示Open hint＋

三次分母对应二阶导数，别漏 2!。

A cubed denominator calls for a second derivative. Remember 2!.

展开分步答案与评分点Open step-by-step solution and rubric＋

ez 在闭圆内解析，1 在圆内。

取柯西高阶公式 ∮f(z)/(z−a)n+1dz=2πif(n)(a)/n!，令 n=2,a=1，故积分为 2πie/2=πie。

也可用 ez=e[1+(z−1)+(z−1)2/2+⋯] 读取留数 e/2。

The function ez is analytic on and inside the circle, and 1 lies inside.

Use the higher-order Cauchy formula ∮f(z)/(z−a)n+1dz=2πif(n)(a)/n! with n=2,a=1.

The integral is 2πie/2=πie.

Alternatively, expand ez=e[1+(z−1)+(z−1)2/2+⋯] and read the residue e/2.

第 5 题Question 512 分 · 傅里叶变换12 points · Fourier transform

指数函数的傅里叶变换Fourier transform of a decaying exponential

采用 F(k)=∫−∞∞f(x)e−ikxdx，求 f(x)=e−a|x|（a>0）的变换，解释为何结果为实偶函数。

With convention F(k)=∫−∞∞f(x)e−ikxdx, find the transform of f(x)=e−a|x| (a>0). Explain why it is real and even.

展开提示Open hint＋

在 x=0 拆开，分别积两个衰减指数。

Split the integral at x=0. Integrate the two exponentials separately.

展开分步答案与评分点Open step-by-step solution and rubric＋

正半轴积分为 1/(a+ik)，负半轴换 t=−x 后为 1/(a−ik)，相加

F(k)=1a+ik+1a−ik=2aa2+k2.

因为 f 实且偶，正弦部分积分为零，余弦部分为实偶函数。

F(0)=2/a 也等于 ∫fdx，可核验系数。

The positive half-line gives 1/(a+ik).

On the negative half-line, substitute t=−x to obtain 1/(a−ik).

Adding gives

F(k)=1a+ik+1a−ik=2aa2+k2.

Since f is real and even, the sine part integrates to zero and the cosine part is real and even.

Also F(0)=2/a equals ∫fdx, which checks the normalization.

核对要点Checkpoints

拆积分4分；积分计算与合并5分；对称性及检验3分。

展开提示Open hint＋

展开分步答案与评分点Open step-by-step solution and rubric＋

  2. 第 2 步Step 2

第 8 题Question 812 分 · Laplace 解 ODE12 points · Laplace transform of an ODE

把初值条件带进变换Carry the initial data into the transform

用拉普拉斯变换解 y′′+2y′+y=1，y(0)=y′(0)=0，t≥0。

Solve by Laplace transform: y′′+2y′+y=1, y(0)=y′(0)=0, t≥0.

展开提示Open hint＋

先变换导数并代入零初值，解得 Y(s)=1/[s(s+1)2]，再拆分。

Transform the derivatives and insert the zero initial data. Solve for Y(s)=1/[s(s+1)2], then use partial fractions.

展开分步答案与评分点Open step-by-step solution and rubric＋

由初值为零，L[y′′]=s2Y、L[y′]=sY，故 (s+1)2Y=1/s。

拆分

Y=1s−1s+1−1(s+1)2.

逆变换得 y=1−e−t−te−t=1−(1+t)e−t。

求导 y′=te−t，所以两初值满足，代回微分方程右侧为1。

The zero initial data give L[y′′]=s2Y and L[y′]=sY, hence (s+1)2Y=1/s.

Decompose

Y=1s−1s+1−1(s+1)2.

The inverse transform gives y=1−e−t−te−t=1−(1+t)e−t.

Differentiation gives y′=te−t, so both initial conditions hold.

Substitution into the differential equation gives the required right-hand side, 1.
