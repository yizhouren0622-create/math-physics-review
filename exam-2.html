第 1 题Question 112 分 · 波动方程12 points · Wave equation

固定弦的单模振动One vibration mode of a fixed string

求 utt=c2uxx，0<x<L，u(0,t)=u(L,t)=0，u(x,0)=Asin(πx/L)，ut(x,0)=0。

Solve utt=c2uxx with 0<x<L, u(0,t)=u(L,t)=0, u(x,0)=Asin(πx/L), and ut(x,0)=0.

展开提示Open hint＋

空间本征函数已经由初位移给定，只需解一个时间模态。

The initial displacement already supplies a spatial mode. Only its time factor remains to be found.

展开分步答案与评分点Open step-by-step solution and rubric＋

取 u=T(t)sin(πx/L)。

代入得 T′′+ω12T=0，ω1=cπ/L。

初值 T(0)=A,T′(0)=0，故

u(x,t)=Acos(cπt/L)sin(πx/L).

边界由正弦保证；位移初值由余弦在0等于1保证；时间导数在0为零。

Set u=T(t)sin(πx/L).

Substitution gives T′′+ω12T=0 and ω1=cπ/L.

The initial data give T(0)=A,T′(0)=0, so

u(x,t)=Acos(cπt/L)sin(πx/L).

The sine factor enforces the endpoints.

The cosine is 1 at time zero, recovering the initial displacement, and its time derivative vanishes there.

核对要点Checkpoints

选空间模态3分；时间方程3分；初值定系数4分；核验2分。

Spatial mode: 3 points; time equation: 3 points; constants: 4 points; verification: 2 points.

第 2 题Question 212 分 · 受迫模态12 points · Forced mode

非共振受迫弦A nonresonant forced string

在同一固定弦上，utt=c2uxx+Fsin(2πx/L)cosΩt，初位移和初速度均为零。设 Ω≥0 且 Ω≠ω2=2πc/L，求解。

A fixed-end string satisfies utt=c2uxx+Fsin(2πx/L)cosΩt with zero initial displacement and velocity. Assume Ω≥0 and Ω≠ω2=2πc/L, and solve.

展开提示Open hint＋

令 u=q(t)sin(2πx/L)，齐次时间项负责抵消初位移。

Set u=q(t)sin(2πx/L). Include a homogeneous term to cancel the particular solution at time zero.

展开分步答案与评分点Open step-by-step solution and rubric＋

代入得 q′′+ω22q=FcosΩt。

特解 FcosΩt/(ω22−Ω2)。

加齐次解 Bcosω2t+Dsinω2t，零初速度给 D=0，零初位移给 B=−F/(ω22−Ω2)。

所以

u=F(cosΩt−cosω2t)ω22−Ω2sin2πxL.

共振不能直接代成 0/0；取极限会得 q=Ftsin(ω2t)/(2ω2)。

Substitution gives q′′+ω22q=FcosΩt.

A particular solution is FcosΩt/(ω22−Ω2).

Add the homogeneous solution Bcosω2t+Dsinω2t; zero initial velocity gives D=0 and zero initial displacement gives B=−F/(ω22−Ω2).

Thus

u=F(cosΩt−cosω2t)ω22−Ω2sin2πxL.

At resonance, do not substitute 0/0 directly into the quotient.

Taking the limit gives q=Ftsin(ω2t)/(2ω2).

模态方程3分；特解与齐次解3分；初值3分；最终式与共振说明3分。

Mode reduction: 3 points; particular and homogeneous solutions: 3 points; initial data: 3 points; result and resonance qualification: 3 points.

第 4 题Question 414 分 · Laplace 分离变量14 points · Laplace equation by separation

矩形稳定场A steady field in a rectangle

矩形 0<x<a,0<y<b 内 uxx+uyy=0，边界 u(0,y)=u(a,y)=u(x,0)=0，u(x,b)=Vsin(πx/a)。求 u。

In rectangle 0<x<a,0<y<b, solve uxx+uyy=0 with boundary data u(0,y)=u(a,y)=u(x,0)=0 and u(x,b)=Vsin(πx/a). Find u.

展开提示Open hint＋

横向正弦、纵向双曲正弦；用顶边定归一化。

Use sine in the horizontal direction and hyperbolic sine vertically. Normalize using the top edge.

展开分步答案与评分点Open step-by-step solution and rubric＋

令 u=X(x)Y(y)，X′′+λX=0，Y′′−λY=0。

左右零边界选 X=sin(πx/a)，λ=(π/a)2。

底边零给 Y=Bsinh(πy/a)，顶边给 B=V/sinh(πb/a)。

故

u=Vsinh(πy/a)sinh(πb/a)sin(πx/a).

对两坐标分别求二阶导数，正负项恰好抵消，四边均满足。

Let u=X(x)Y(y).

Then X′′+λX=0 and Y′′−λY=0.

The zero left and right edges select X=sin(πx/a) and λ=(π/a)2.

The bottom edge gives Y=Bsinh(πy/a); the top gives B=V/sinh(πb/a).

Hence

u=Vsinh(πy/a)sinh(πb/a)sin(πx/a).

The second derivatives in the two coordinates cancel, and all four boundary conditions hold.

核对要点Checkpoints

分离符号与本征函数4分；纵向解4分；边界定系数4分；验证2分。

Separation signs and eigenvalue: 4 points; vertical solution: 4 points; boundary coefficient: 4 points; check: 2 points.

第 5 题Question 512 分 · Sturm–Liouville12 points · Sturm–Liouville

混合边界的本征值Eigenvalues with mixed boundary conditions

求 −X′′=λX，X′(0)=0,X(L)=0 的全部本征值、本征函数及其平方范数。

Find all eigenvalues, eigenfunctions, and squared norms for −X′′=λX, X′(0)=0,X(L)=0.

展开提示Open hint＋

正本征值给余弦；零或负值分别排除。

For a positive eigenvalue start with cosine. Exclude zero and negative eigenvalues separately.

展开分步答案与评分点Open step-by-step solution and rubric＋

若 λ=k2>0，通解 Acoskx+Bsinkx。

X′(0)=0 给 B=0，X(L)=0 给 coskL=0。

所以

λn=((n+1/2)π/L)2,Xn=cos((n+1/2)πx/L),n=0,1,….

λ=0 时 X=A+Bx，两边界只得零解；λ=−μ2 时 X=Acoshμx，因 coshμL>0 也只有零解。

积分 ∫0LXn2dx=L/2。

不同频率用积化和差可验正交。

For λ=k2>0 the general solution is Acoskx+Bsinkx.

Condition X′(0)=0 gives B=0, and X(L)=0 gives coskL=0.

Therefore

λn=((n+1/2)π/L)2,Xn=cos((n+1/2)πx/L),n=0,1,….

For λ=0, solution X=A+Bx satisfies both boundaries only when it is zero.

For λ=−μ2, use X=Acoshμx; since coshμL>0, only the zero solution remains.

The norm integral is ∫0LXn2dx=L/2.

Product-to-sum identities also verify orthogonality of different frequencies.

核对要点Checkpoints

正值求谱5分；排除零负值4分；范数3分。

Positive eigenvalues: 5 points; exclusion of zero and negative values: 4 points; norm: 3 points.

第 6 题Question 612 分 · 级数递推12 points · Coefficient recurrence

普通点幂级数Power series at an ordinary point

用幂级数解 y′′−xy=0，y(0)=1,y′(0)=0，写出至 x9 项。

Solve y′′−xy=0, y(0)=1,y′(0)=0 by power series through x9.

展开提示Open hint＋

设 y=∑anxn，比较系数时把 xy 下标平移。

Set y=∑anxn. Shift the index in xy before comparing powers.

展开分步答案与评分点Open step-by-step solution and rubric＋

y′′=∑n≥0(n+2)(n+1)an+2xn，xy=∑n≥1an−1xn。

常数项给 a2=0；n≥1 给 an+2=an−1/[(n+2)(n+1)]。

由 a0=1,a1=0 得 a3=1/6,a6=1/180,a9=1/12960，其他至9次项为零。

y=1+x36+x6180+x912960+O(x12).

每隔3阶形成一条递推链，初值选中了其中一条。

We have y′′=∑n≥0(n+2)(n+1)an+2xn and xy=∑n≥1an−1xn.

The constant coefficient gives a2=0; coefficient n≥1 gives an+2=an−1/[(n+2)(n+1)].

From a0=1,a1=0 we obtain a3=1/6,a6=1/180,a9=1/12960; the other coefficients through degree 9 vanish.

y=1+x36+x6180+x912960+O(x12).

The recurrence splits into chains spaced three indices apart.

The initial data select one chain.

第 8 题Question 812 分 · Dirichlet 镜像法12 points · Dirichlet method of images

镜像与零边界格林函数Images and a zero-boundary Green function

上半空间 z>0 中，取约定 −∇2G(r,r0)=δ(r−r0)，边界 G=0 于 z=0，并要求 |r|→∞ 时 G→0。写出格林函数，并说明两项分别起什么作用。

In the upper half-space z>0, use convention −∇2G(r,r0)=δ(r−r0), with G=0 on z=0 and G→0 as |r|→∞. Write the Green function and explain the role of each term.

展开提示Open hint＋

真实源在域内，负镜像源在域外。

Place the real source inside and its negative image outside.

展开分步答案与评分点Open step-by-step solution and rubric＋

令 r0=(x0,y0,z0)，z0>0，镜像点 r0*=(x0,y0,−z0)。

自由空间基本解为 1/(4π|r−r0|)，故

G=14π|r−r0|−14π|r−r0*|.

在域内，第二项没有奇点且调和，第一项提供单位源；在 z=0 两距离相等，恰好相消。

两项在无穷远趋零。

若方程约定改为 +∇2G=δ，整体应取负号。

Let r0=(x0,y0,z0) and z0>0, with reflected point r0*=(x0,y0,−z0).

The free-space fundamental solution is 1/(4π|r−r0|), so

G=14π|r−r0|−14π|r−r0*|.

Inside the domain, the second term has no singularity and is harmonic; the first supplies the unit source.

On z=0 the two distances agree, so the terms cancel.

Both terms decay at infinity.

If the convention becomes +∇2G=δ, reverse the overall sign.

核对要点Checkpoints

源与镜像点3分；格林函数4分；域内和边界检查3分；符号约定2分。

Source and image positions: 3 points; Green function: 4 points; interior and boundary checks: 3 points; sign convention: 2 points.
