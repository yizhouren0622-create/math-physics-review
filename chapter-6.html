格林函数：算符对点源的响应Green functions: the response to a point source

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

格林函数先回答“只在一个点放入单位源，会发生什么”。一般分布的源再由这些点源响应叠加得到；第一步先固定方程的正负号。

A Green function is the response to a unit source at one point. Add weighted point-source responses to solve for a general source. Fix the operator’s sign convention first.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

把一般源分解成点源，先求算符对一个点源的响应，再积分叠加。本章采用教材约定 ΔG=δ；若改用 −ΔG=δ，所有格林函数整体变号。

Imagine building a general source from many tiny point sources. First find the response to one unit point source, then add the responses by integration. This chapter follows the textbook convention ΔG=δ; with the alternative convention −ΔG=δ, every Green function changes its overall sign.

ΔGd=δ,G1=|x−x0|2,G2=ln|r−r0|2π,G3=−14π|r−r0|
使用条件When it applies

等式在分布意义成立，源点以外均为调和函数。无界域须额外说明无穷远条件；一维基本解还可加任意仿射函数，二维需约定对数中的参考长度。

The equality holds in the sense of distributions: the point source is included, while the function is harmonic away from it. An unbounded domain also needs a condition at infinity. In one dimension any affine function may be added; in two dimensions the logarithm needs a reference length.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

三维球对称调和函数为 A+B/r。围绕源点的小球上，∫ΔGdV=∮∂rGdS=−4πB，令其等于 1，得到 B=−1/(4π)。二维环向通量为 2πr∂rG=1，积分得 lnr/(2π)。一维对源点两侧积分得到导数跳跃 1，而 |x−x0|/2 的左右导数恰为 −1/2,1/2。

In three dimensions, a spherically symmetric harmonic function has the form A+B/r. On a small sphere around the source, ∫ΔGdV=∮∂rGdS=−4πB. A unit source means this flux must be 1, giving B=−1/(4π). In two dimensions the outward flux is 2πr∂rG=1; integrating gives lnr/(2π). In one dimension, integrating across the source requires a derivative jump of 1. The left and right derivatives of |x−x0|/2 are exactly −1/2,1/2.

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

为什么 1/(4πr) 不符合本章基本解的符号？

Why is 1/(4πr) the wrong sign for this chapter's fundamental solution?

解答

Solution

它在小球上的外向通量是 −1，因此满足 Δ[1/(4πr)]=−δ。先写清算符与点源的正负号再套公式。

Its outward flux through a small sphere is −1, so it satisfies Δ[1/(4πr)]=−δ. Before using a memorized formula, write down the signs in the operator and the point source.

记忆线索Memory cue

一维折线、二维对数、三维倒数；先做通量检查正负号。

One dimension: a V-shaped line. Two dimensions: a logarithm. Three dimensions: an inverse distance. Check the sign with flux.

Dirichlet 格林函数与边界积分Dirichlet Green functions and boundary integrals

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

边界值已知时，要让格林函数在边界上为零。简单平面可用镜像相消；写完后检查源点、边界和无穷远。

For prescribed boundary values, use a Green function that vanishes on the boundary. Images work for simple planes. Check the source singularity, boundary, and infinity.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

格林函数既要产生单位点源，还要满足齐次边界条件。镜像源放在求解区域之外，目的在于抵消边界值，不能在区域内部额外制造奇点。

A Green function must produce one unit point source and satisfy a zero boundary condition. An image source is an imaginary helper placed outside the region being solved. Its job is to cancel the boundary value; it must not introduce another singularity inside the region.

u(r)=∫VG(r,r′)f(r′)dV′+∫∂Vφ(r′)∂n′G(r,r′)dS′
使用条件When it applies

Δr′G=δ(r′−r)，G|∂V=0，Δu=f，u|∂V=φ；n′ 是区域的外法向。外域另需足够的无穷远条件。

Δr′G=δ(r′−r), G|∂V=0, Δu=f, and u|∂V=φ. Here n′ points out of the region. An exterior domain also needs suitable conditions at infinity.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

由第二格林公式 ∫V(uΔG−GΔu)=∮(u∂nG−G∂nu)，代入点源与齐次 Dirichlet 边界，左边成为 u(r)−∫Gf，右边仅剩 ∮φ∂nG，移项即得。源点奇性可通过挖去小球再取极限严谨处理。

Start with Green's second identity, ∫V(uΔG−GΔu)=∮(u∂nG−G∂nu). Insert the point-source equation and the zero Dirichlet condition for G. The left side becomes u(r)−∫Gf; only ∮φ∂nG remains on the right. Move the source integral to the right to obtain the formula. For a rigorous treatment of the singularity, remove a small sphere around the source and then let its radius tend to zero.

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

上半空间、零边界、单位点源的格林函数如何构造？

How do we construct the Green function for a unit point source in the upper half-space with zero boundary values?

解答

Solution

令源点为 (x0,y0,z0)，y0>0，镜像点为 (x0,−y0,z0)。取 G=−1/(4πR)+1/(4πR*)。平面上两距离相等，边界为零；镜像点不在上半空间，因此域内只有真实点源。

Put the source at (x0,y0,z0), y0>0, and its image at (x0,−y0,z0). Choose G=−1/(4πR)+1/(4πR*). On the plane, the two distances are equal, so the boundary value is zero. The image is outside the upper half-space, leaving only the real point source inside.

记忆线索Memory cue

先对算符，再对边界，再对无穷远；表面积分用外法向。

Check the operator, then the boundary, then infinity. The surface integral uses the outward normal.

不同边值问题的格林函数Green functions for different boundary conditions

本节无书后习题No end-of-section exercises
知识点 Concept 理解后再记忆Understand before memorizing

Neumann 相容条件、常数自由度与 Robin 边界Neumann compatibility, the arbitrary constant, and Robin boundaries

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

只给边界上的流量，温度整体加一个常数通常不受影响。先检查总源强与总流量是否平衡；Robin边界还要额外检查是否出现特殊参数。

Flux data usually leave an additive constant undetermined. Check the balance of total source and boundary flux first. Robin conditions also require checking exceptional parameters.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

给定法向导数并不确定整体常数。更关键的是源的总量必须等于流出边界的总通量。有界域中不能同时要求 ΔG=δ 和处处零法向导数。

Prescribing the normal derivative does not fix the overall constant in u. Before solving, check that the total source equals the total outward boundary flux. In a bounded domain, a Green function cannot satisfy both ΔG=δ and zero normal derivative everywhere.

∫VfdV=∮∂VgdS,ΔGN=δ−1|V|,∂nGN=0
使用条件When it applies

域连通且有界，Δu=f、∂nu=g。修正型 Neumann 格林函数另用 ∫VGN=0 固定常数；也可保持 ΔG=δ 而给 G 非零的相容通量。Robin 条件应检查齐次问题是否有非零解。

The domain is connected and bounded, with Δu=f and ∂nu=g. For the modified Neumann Green function, impose ∫VGN=0 to fix its constant. Alternatively, keep ΔG=δ and give G a compatible nonzero boundary flux. For a Robin condition, check whether the homogeneous problem has a nonzero solution.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

对 Δu=f 积分并用散度定理，得到相容条件。若两个解有相同 Neumann 数据，其差 w 满足 Δw=0,∂nw=0；分部积分给 ∫|∇w|2=0，故仅差常数。若强令 ΔG=δ,∂nG=0，积分得到 1=0；减去 1/|V| 后总源量为零，矛盾解除。此时格林公式求得 u−u¯，仍需指定平均值。

Integrate Δu=f and apply the divergence theorem to obtain the compatibility condition. If two solutions have identical Neumann data, their difference w satisfies Δw=0,∂nw=0. Integration by parts gives ∫|∇w|2=0, so w is constant. If we incorrectly require ΔG=δ,∂nG=0, integration gives 1=0. Subtracting 1/|V| makes the total source zero and removes the contradiction. Green's identity then determines u−u¯; the mean value must still be specified.

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

区间 (0,L) 上 u′′=1、u′(0)=u′(L)=0 有解吗？

Does u′′=1 on (0,L) with u′(0)=u′(L)=0 have a solution?

解答

Solution

无解。积分得 u′(L)−u′(0)=L，而边界给左边为 0。若改为 u′(L)=L 则有解 u=x2/2+C，仍需给一个值固定 C。

No. Integrating gives u′(L)−u′(0)=L, but the boundary data make the left side 0. If the second condition is changed to u′(L)=L, a solution is u=x2/2+C. One additional value is still needed to fix C.

记忆线索Memory cue

纯通量边界：先算总量，再谈解；加常数不改变通量。

For flux-only boundaries, check the total balance first. Adding a constant does not change the flux.

亥姆霍兹基本解与辐射条件Helmholtz fundamental solutions and radiation conditions

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

同一频率下的波场需要区分向外传播与向内传播。先固定时间因子的约定，再用辐射条件选择正确的指数符号。

A single-frequency wave field must distinguish outgoing from incoming waves. Fix the time convention, then use the radiation condition to select the exponential sign.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

对空间简谐波，径向基本解是球面波除以距离。正负指数都满足同一个点源方程，区别来自辐射条件；判断出射和入射还必须说明时间因子。

For a time-harmonic wave, the radial fundamental solution is a spherical wave divided by distance. Both signs in the exponential satisfy the same point-source equation. The radiation condition selects one sign, and the time factor tells us which wave is outgoing.

(Δ+k2)G±=δ,G±(R)=−e±ikR4πR
使用条件When it applies

k>0，三维无界均匀空间。在时间因子 e−iωt 下，e+ikR/R 为出射波，满足 R(∂RG−ikG)→0；若时间因子改为 e+iωt，命名反转。

k>0 in uniform, unbounded three-dimensional space. With the time factor e−iωt, e+ikR/R is outgoing and satisfies R(∂RG−ikG)→0. If the time factor is e+iωt, the outgoing/incoming names are reversed.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

源点外令 v=RG，径向方程化为 v′′+k2v=0，所以 G=(AeikR+Be−ikR)/R。每个 G± 在原点附近均为 −1/(4πR)+O(1)，小球外向通量趋于 1，且 k2∫G→0，因此产生单位 δ 源。辐射条件在这两类解中选择所需的一类。

Away from the source, set v=RG. The radial equation becomes v′′+k2v=0, so G=(AeikR+Be−ikR)/R. Near the origin, both G± have the form −1/(4πR)+O(1). Their outward flux through a small sphere tends to 1, while k2∫G→0, so each produces a unit δ source. The radiation condition chooses the required wave from these two possibilities.

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

为什么只验 R>0 的微分方程不足以证明基本解？

Why is checking the differential equation only for R>0 insufficient?

解答

Solution

因为任何常数倍的 eikR/R 在源点外都满足齐次方程。必须再用小球通量确定点源的系数为 1。

Any constant multiple of eikR/R satisfies the homogeneous equation away from the source. The small-sphere flux is also needed to show that the point-source coefficient is exactly 1.

记忆线索Memory cue

径向乘 R 变成简谐方程；δ 的系数用通量定。

Multiply the radial function by R to get a harmonic-oscillator equation. Use flux to fix the δ coefficient.

推迟格林函数与因果响应Retarded Green functions and causal response

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

一个点发生的变化，要过一段传播时间才影响远处。把源项的时间向前回溯“距离除以速度”，就体现了因果关系。

A disturbance takes time to reach a distant point. Evaluate the source at an earlier time, delayed by distance divided by speed, to enforce causality.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

波动信息以有限速度传播：观察点当前的响应，来自源点在 R/c 之前的作用。推迟解要求响应不早于源的激发。

A wave travels at a finite speed. The response observed now comes from what the source did R/c earlier. A retarded solution is causal: it cannot respond before the source acts or before the signal arrives.

Gret=δ(t−t′−R/c)4πc2R,R=|r−r′|
使用条件When it applies

此处算符为 ∂t2−c2Δ，三维无界空间，零初始响应。若算符写成 c−2∂t2−Δ，格林函数相应乘 c2；初始位移、速度不为零时要另加齐次初值解。

Here the operator is ∂t2−c2Δ in unbounded three-dimensional space, with zero initial response. For the operator c−2∂t2−Δ, multiply the Green function by c2. Nonzero initial displacement or velocity requires an additional homogeneous initial-value solution.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

对空间作 Fourier 变换，点源响应满足 G^tt+c2|k|2G^=δ(t−t′)。因果解为 H(τ)sin(c|k|τ)/(c|k|)；其本身连续、时间导数在 τ=0 跳跃 1。三维逆变换得到 δ(τ−R/c)/(4πc2R)。将它与源积分，δ 自动选出推迟时刻。

Take a spatial Fourier transform. The point-source response satisfies G^tt+c2|k|2G^=δ(t−t′). Its causal solution is H(τ)sin(c|k|τ)/(c|k|): the function is continuous, while its time derivative jumps by 1 at τ=0. The three-dimensional inverse transform gives δ(τ−R/c)/(4πc2R). In the source integral, δ automatically picks out the retarded time.

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

原点从 t=0 开始持续施加源 f(r,t)=δ(r)H(t)，零初值响应是什么？

A source f(r,t)=δ(r)H(t) starts acting continuously at the origin at t=0. What is the response with zero initial data?

解答

Solution

u(R,t)=H(t−R/c)/(4πc2R)。在 t<R/c 时尚未到达；到达后取上述静态强度。源点处需按分布理解。

u(R,t)=H(t−R/c)/(4πc2R). For t<R/c the signal has not arrived; afterward the response has the stated static strength. At the source itself, interpret the expression as a distribution.

记忆线索Memory cue

先选因果方向；把源的 t 换成 t−R/c；检查算符前的 c²。

Choose the causal direction; replace the source time t by t−R/c; check the c² factor in the operator.

一维无界空间的拉普拉斯基本解The Laplacian fundamental solution on the whole line

求一维无界空间上拉普拉斯算子的格林函数。

Find the Green function of the Laplacian on the unbounded one-dimensional line.

读题注意Read this first

无界一维问题未给唯一性条件。标准基本解只是一个规范选择，不能额外强加两端同时衰减。

The whole-line problem does not specify uniqueness conditions. The standard fundamental solution is a normalization choice; imposing decay at both ends would be impossible.

这道题要你做什么What are you trying to find?

找到一个函数：除源点外不弯曲，在源点产生一个单位点源。

Find a function that is straight away from the source and creates one unit point source at the source.

看到什么，就想到这个方法Clue → choice of method

“一维拉普拉斯”就是二阶导数。δ 表示集中在一个点、总量为 1 的源，所以从源点两侧分别求解。

In one dimension, the Laplacian is the second derivative. δ means a source concentrated at one point with total strength 1, so solve on the two sides separately.

不会做？带我一步一步做Guide me, one step at a time＋

  1. 先写 G′′=δ(x−x0)。在 x<x0 和 x>x0 两边都令二阶导数为 0，得到两段直线。

    Write G′′=δ(x−x0). For both x<x0 and x>x0, set the second derivative to zero: each piece is a straight line.

  2. 两段直线在源点接上。若函数本身跳跃，会多出题目没有的 δ′。

    Join the lines continuously. A jump in G itself would create a δ′ term that the equation does not contain.

  3. 对源点附近积分：右斜率减左斜率必须等于 1。对称选择 G=A|x−x0|，所以 2A=1。

    Integrate across the source: right slope minus left slope must be 1. For the symmetric choice G=A|x−x0|, this gives 2A=1.

  4. 写出 G=|x−x0|/2。最后注明还可加 Ax+B，题目没给额外条件，不能说只有这一个。

    Write G=|x−x0|/2. State that Ax+B may also be added; without extra conditions, this choice is not unique.

停一下，试着回答Pause and check

把 G 改成 −|x−x0|/2，右斜率减左斜率是多少？

For G=−|x−x0|/2, what is the right slope minus the left slope?

核对这个小问题Check this answer

−1，所以它对应负单位源，不符合本题。

−1. It represents a negative unit source and does not solve this problem.

提示：从哪里入手Hint: where to start＋

对 G′′=δ(x−x0) 在源点附近积分，得到 G′(x0+)−G′(x0−)=1。

Integrate G′′=δ(x−x0) across the source to get G′(x0+)−G′(x0−)=1.

查看完整计算与答案Open the full calculation and answer＋

解题思路

Plan

把实轴在源点处分成两段：各段解齐次方程，用连续性与导数跳跃连接。

Split the line at the source. Solve the homogeneous equation on each side, then join the two pieces using continuity and a derivative jump.

逐步解答

Step-by-step solution

  1. 源点以外 G′′=0，故两侧均为仿射函数。若 G 本身跳跃，则二阶导数出现 δ′；题目只有 δ，因此 G 应连续。

  2. 取关于源点对称、平移不变的基本解 G=A|x−x0|+B。两侧导数为 −A、A，导数跳跃为 2A，必须等于 1，故 A=1/2。令参考常数 B=0。

  3. 在分布意义下 (|x−x0|/2)′=sgn(x−x0)/2，再求导等于 δ，验证完毕。

  4. 最一般的基本解可加齐次解：G=|x−x0|/2+Ax+B。一维不存在两端都趋零的此类基本解；两端斜率若都为零便不可能有单位跳跃。题目没有附加条件时，不能声称唯一。

  1. Away from the source, G′′=0, so G is affine on each side. A jump in G itself would produce δ′ in its second derivative. The equation contains only δ, so G must be continuous.

  2. Choose a symmetric, translation-invariant fundamental solution G=A|x−x0|+B. Its left and right derivatives are −A and A. Their jump is 2A, which must equal 1; therefore A=1/2. Choose the reference constant B=0.

  3. In the distributional sense, (|x−x0|/2)′=sgn(x−x0)/2. Differentiating once more gives δ, completing the check.

  4. Any homogeneous solution may be added: G=|x−x0|/2+Ax+B. No such one-dimensional fundamental solution tends to zero at both ends: zero slopes at both ends cannot produce a unit jump. Without extra conditions, the solution is not unique.

最终答案

Final answer

G(x,x0)=|x−x0|/2 是标准对称基本解；一般可加 Ax+B。

  3. 在 r=0 附近展开 e±iωr，两种 G 的首项都是 −1/(4πr)。

    Expand e±iωr near r=0. Both G functions start with −1/(4πr).

  4. 对半径 ε 的小球算通量：4πε2∂rG→1；另一个体积分项趋零，所以点源强度为 1。

    Calculate flux through a sphere of radius ε: 4πε2∂rG→1. The other volume integral tends to zero, so the point-source strength is 1.

  5. 最后写明：辐射条件选择正负号；用哪个时间因子，会影响“出射”的命名。

    State that the radiation condition chooses the sign; the time factor affects which sign is called outgoing.

If G is multiplied by 2, does the equation away from the source still hold? Is the source still a unit source?

核对这个小问题Check this answer

源点外仍成立，但小球通量变成 2，所以产生的是两倍点源。

The equation away from the source still holds, but the small-sphere flux becomes 2: the point source has double strength.

解题思路

Plan

分开验证源点外的齐次方程与源点上的 δ 归一化。

Check two separate things: the homogeneous equation away from the source, and the δ normalization at the source.

逐步解答

Step-by-step solution

  1. 对 r>0，令 v±=rG±=−e±iωr/(4π)。直接求导得 v±′′=−ω2v±，因此 ΔG±+ω2G±=0。

  2. r→0 时展开：G±=−1/(4πr)∓iω/(4π)+O(r)，两式具有相同的奇异主部。故 ∂rG±=1/(4πr2)+O(1)，在半径 ε 的小球上

    ∮r=ε∂rG±dS=1+O(ε2)→1.
    同时 ω2∫r<εG±dV=O(ε2)→0。

  3. 因而该算符在源点产生单位点源，在其余位置为零。更严格地对光滑紧支试验函数 ψ，在挖去小球的区域分部积分，内边界极限仅留下 ψ(0)，于是 ⟨(Δ+ω2)G±,ψ⟩=ψ(0)。二者均满足所求分布方程。

  4. 两解之差 G+−G−=−isin(ωr)/(2πr) 在原点有有限极限，满足齐次方程。方程本身不能选择正负号，需要辐射条件。时间因子 e−iωt 时 + 为出射；教材若采用 e+iωt，出入射称呼反向。

  1. For r>0, set v±=rG±=−e±iωr/(4π). Direct differentiation gives v±′′=−ω2v±, so ΔG±+ω2G±=0.

  2. Near r=0, expand: G±=−1/(4πr)∓iω/(4π)+O(r). Both have the same singular leading term. Thus ∂rG±=1/(4πr2)+O(1), and on a sphere of radius ε,

    ∮r=ε∂rG±dS=1+O(ε2)→1.
    Also ω2∫r<εG±dV=O(ε2)→0.

  3. The operator therefore produces a unit point source at the origin and zero elsewhere. More rigorously, take a smooth compactly supported test function ψ and integrate by parts in the region with a small sphere removed. The inner-boundary limit leaves only ψ(0), giving ⟨(Δ+ω2)G±,ψ⟩=ψ(0). Both functions satisfy the required distributional equation.

  4. Their difference G+−G−=−isin(ωr)/(2πr) has a finite limit at the origin and satisfies the homogeneous equation. The equation alone cannot select the sign; a radiation condition is needed. With the time factor e−iωt, + is outgoing. If the textbook uses e+iωt, the outgoing/incoming names are reversed.

最终答案

Final answer

两种函数均满足 (Δ+ω2)G=δ；源点外为齐次解，小球通量均趋于 1。

Both functions satisfy (Δ+ω2)G=δ: each solves the homogeneous equation away from the source and has small-sphere flux tending to 1.

可复用步骤

Reusable steps

径向乘 r 降阶→求二阶导验齐次→展开奇异主部→通量定 δ 系数→说明辐射条件。

Multiply the radial function by r → differentiate twice to check the homogeneous equation → expand the singular part → fix the δ coefficient by flux → state the radiation condition.

易错点

Common mistakes

只算 r>0 不能证明非齐次点源方程；出射/入射命名依赖时间约定，不能只看空间指数。

A calculation only for r>0 cannot prove an inhomogeneous point-source equation. Outgoing/incoming names depend on the time convention, not just on the spatial exponential.
