格林函数：算符对点源的响应Green functions: the response to a point source

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

格林函数先回答“只在一个点放入单位源，会发生什么”。一般分布的源再由这些点源响应叠加得到；第一步先固定方程的正负号。

A Green function is the response to a unit source at one point. Add weighted point-source responses to solve for a general source. Fix the operator’s sign convention first.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

把一般源分解成点源，先求算符对一个点源的响应，再积分叠加。本章采用教材约定 ΔG=δ；若改用 −ΔG=δ，所有格林函数整体变号。

Imagine building a general source from many tiny point sources. First find the response to one unit point source, then add the responses by integration. This chapter follows the textbook convention ΔG=δ; with the alternative convention −ΔG=δ, every Green function changes its overall sign.

ΔGd=δ,G1=|x−x0|2,G2=ln|r−r0|2π,G3=−14π|r−r0|
使用条件When it applies

等式在分布意义成立，源点以外均为调和函数。无界域须额外说明无穷远条件；一维基本解还可加任意仿射函数，二维需约定对数中的参考长度。

The equality holds in the sense of distributions: the point source is included, while the function is harmonic away from it. An unbounded domain also needs a condition at infinity. In one dimension any affine function may be added; in two dimensions the logarithm needs a reference length.

展开分步证明 / 推导Open the step-by-step proof / derivation＋

先固定符号 ΔG=δ。

把源点附近任意小体积中的方程积分，右边总是 1；散度定理把左边变成边界外向通量，所以基本解的关键是“源点外调和，小球通量为 1”。

下面给出三种维数的核心计算，点源等式按分布理解。

三维令 R=|r−r0|，球对称源外方程是 (R2G′)′=0。

积分得 G=A+B/R，于是 G′=−B/R2。

乘球面积 4πR2 得通量 −4πB=1，所以 B=−1/(4π)；取三维无穷远零值时 A=0。

二维圆对称源外方程是 (RG′)′=0，积分得 G=A+BlnR。

圆周外向通量为 2πRG′=2πB，故 B=1/(2π)。

严格写成 ln(R/R*)/(2π)，其中参考长度 R* 只改变一个常数；二维对数基本解不能像三维那样在无穷远趋零。

一维在源点两侧有 G′′=0，因此各为直线；函数连续以避免额外的 δ′。

跨源点积分给 G′(x0+)−G′(x0−)=1，对称选择 G=|x−x0|/2 的两斜率为 1/2,−1/2，正好符合。

任意 Ax+B 仍可加入；三种形式的符号由通量或斜率跳跃决定，不能靠记忆统一套负号。

First fix the sign convention ΔG=δ.

Integrating over any small neighborhood of the source gives 1 on the right.

The divergence theorem converts the left side to outward boundary flux.

A fundamental solution must therefore be harmonic away from the source and have unit small-boundary flux.

The following calculations cover three dimensions of space, with the point-source equality interpreted distributionally.

In three dimensions, let R=|r−r0|.

The radial equation away from the source is (R2G′)′=0.

Integrating gives G=A+B/R, so G′=−B/R2.

Multiplying by the sphere area 4πR2 gives flux −4πB=1, hence B=−1/(4π).

Choosing zero value at three-dimensional infinity sets A=0.

In two dimensions, the radial source-free equation is (RG′)′=0, giving G=A+BlnR.

The outward circular flux is 2πRG′=2πB, hence B=1/(2π).

Write the logarithm as ln(R/R*)/(2π) if a dimensionless argument is needed.

The reference length R* changes only a constant; unlike the three-dimensional inverse distance, this logarithmic fundamental solution cannot decay at infinity.

In one dimension, G′′=0 on both sides of the source, so each side is a straight line.

Continuity avoids an extra δ′.

Integration across the source gives G′(x0+)−G′(x0−)=1.

The symmetric choice G=|x−x0|/2 has right and left slopes 1/2,−1/2, as required.

Any Ax+B may still be added.

In all three cases, flux or a slope jump fixes the sign; one cannot attach the same remembered negative sign in every dimension.

题目Problem

为什么 1/(4πr) 不符合本章 ΔG=δ 的基本解符号？

Why does 1/(4πr) have the wrong sign for this chapter's convention ΔG=δ?

已知什么Given

r=|r|，候选函数为 g(r)=1/(4πr)。点源系数可由包围原点的小球通量求出。

r=|r|, and the candidate is g(r)=1/(4πr). The point-source coefficient can be found from the flux through a small sphere around the origin.

要求什么Find

判断 Δg 的符号，并写出满足 ΔG=δ 的 G。

Determine the sign of Δg, then satisfy ΔG=δ by writing G.

  1. 第 1 步Step 1先查源点外Check away from the source
    为什么这样做Why this step

    基本解必须在没有点源的地方满足齐次方程。

    A fundamental solution must satisfy the homogeneous equation wherever there is no point source.

    具体怎么算Calculation

    对 r>0，径向公式给 Δg=r−2(r2g′)′=r−2[−1/(4π)]′=0。

    For r>0, the radial formula gives Δg=r−2(r2g′)′=r−2[−1/(4π)]′=0.

  2. 第 2 步Step 2计算径向导数Differentiate radially
    为什么这样做Why this step

    穿过小球面的通量就是点源强度。

    The flux through a small sphere is the strength of the point source.

    具体怎么算Calculation

    g′(r)=−1/(4πr2)；半径为 ε 的球面面积是 4πε2。

    g′(r)=−1/(4πr2), while a sphere of radius ε has area 4πε2.

  3. 第 3 步Step 3求小球通量Compute the small-sphere flux
    为什么这样做Why this step

    散度定理把 Δg 在小球内的积分变成边界通量。

    The divergence theorem converts the integral of Δg over the ball into boundary flux.

    具体怎么算Calculation

    ∫r<εΔgdV=4πε2g′(ε)=4πε2[−1/(4πε2)]=−1。

  4. 第 4 步Step 4修正整体符号Correct the overall sign
    为什么这样做Why this step

    通量为 −1 表示候选函数产生的是负单位点源。

    Flux −1 means that the candidate produces a negative unit point source.

    具体怎么算Calculation

    因此 Δg=−δ；把函数整体乘 −1，得到 G(r)=−1/(4πr) 且 ΔG=δ。

    Therefore Δg=−δ. Multiplying the function by −1 gives G(r)=−1/(4πr) and ΔG=δ.

做完以后这样检查Check after solving

  • G′(r)=1/(4πr2)，故小球通量为 4πε2G′(ε)=1。

    G′(r)=1/(4πr2), so its small-sphere flux is 4πε2G′(ε)=1.

  • 对任意 r>0，G 仍满足 ΔG=0；点源只在 r=0。

    For every r>0, G still satisfies ΔG=0; the point source occurs only at r=0.

  • 若题目改用算符 −Δ，则 1/(4πr) 的正号反而正确。

    If the operator were −Δ, the positive sign in 1/(4πr) would instead be correct.

在 x≠2 处没有源，所以 u′′=0。

两侧分别是仿射函数；关于源点对称并且连续时，可以统一写成 u=C|x−2|+B。

跨过源点积分，得到 u′(2+)−u′(2−)=3。

右斜率是 C，左斜率是 −C，故 2C=3，即 C=3/2。

它的导数跳跃正好产生强度为 3 的点源。

方程本身可以加任意仿射齐次解；本题的对称条件去掉非零斜率项，参考条件再固定常数。

不能另外要求两端都衰减，因为这个解随离源距离线性增长。

For x≠2 there is no source, so u′′=0.

Each piece is affine.

Symmetry about the source and continuity let us combine them as u=C|x−2|+B.

Integrating across the source gives u′(2+)−u′(2−)=3.

The right slope is C and the left slope is −C, hence 2C=3, or C=3/2.

The reference condition u(2)=0 gives B=0, so u=3|x−2|/2.

Its slope jump produces exactly a point source of strength 3.

The differential equation alone allows any affine homogeneous addition.

Here symmetry removes a nonzero slope addition and the reference condition fixes the constant.

Dirichlet 格林函数与边界积分Dirichlet Green functions and boundary integrals

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

边界值已知时，要让格林函数在边界上为零。简单平面可用镜像相消；写完后检查源点、边界和无穷远。

For prescribed boundary values, use a Green function that vanishes on the boundary. Images work for simple planes. Check the source singularity, boundary, and infinity.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

格林函数既要产生单位点源，还要满足齐次边界条件。镜像源放在求解区域之外，目的在于抵消边界值，不能在区域内部额外制造奇点。

A Green function must produce one unit point source and satisfy a zero boundary condition. An image source is an imaginary helper placed outside the region being solved. Its job is to cancel the boundary value; it must not introduce another singularity inside the region.

u(r)=∫VG(r,r′)f(r′)dV′+∫∂Vφ(r′)∂n′G(r,r′)dS′
使用条件When it applies

Δr′G=δ(r′−r)，G|∂V=0，Δu=f，u|∂V=φ；n′ 是区域的外法向。外域另需足够的无穷远条件。

Δr′G=δ(r′−r), G|∂V=0, Δu=f, and u|∂V=φ. Here n′ points out of the region. An exterior domain also needs suitable conditions at infinity.

展开分步证明 / 推导Open the step-by-step proof / derivation＋

固定观察点 r，把带撇号的 r′ 当积分变量。

选 Δr′G=δ(r′−r) 且 G=0 于边界：前者保证读出观察点的值，后者用来消掉未知边界导数。

这两个要求各有作用，缺一不可。

从第二格林公式 ∫V(uΔG−GΔu)dV′=∮∂V(u∂n′G−G∂n′u)dS′ 出发。

代入 Δu=f，再用点源的取值性质 ∫u(r′)δ(r′−r)dV′=u(r)，左边成为 u(r)−∫VGfdV′。

在边界，G=0 消掉原本不知道的 ∂n′u 项，已知 u=φ 则留下 ∮φ∂n′GdS′。

把体源项移到右边，得到 u=∫VGfdV′+∮φ∂n′GdS′；两项分别对应源与边界输入。

这是可跟算的核心推导。

严谨处理奇点时，先挖去观察点的小球，再分部积分并取球半径趋零；小球边界给出上述 u(r)。

镜像法只是构造合适 G 的办法，像点必须在域外；无界域还要检查无穷远边界项消失及积分收敛，法向始终取求解域的外法向。

Fix the observation point r and use r′ as the integration variable.

Choose Δr′G=δ(r′−r) and G=0 on the boundary.

The first condition extracts the observation value; the second removes an unknown boundary derivative.

Both are needed.

Start from Green’s second identity: ∫V(uΔG−GΔu)dV′=∮∂V(u∂n′G−G∂n′u)dS′.

Insert Δu=f and the sampling property ∫u(r′)δ(r′−r)dV′=u(r).

The left side becomes u(r)−∫VGfdV′.

On the boundary, G=0 removes the otherwise unknown ∂n′u term, while the known value u=φ leaves ∮φ∂n′GdS′.

Move the source term to the right to get u=∫VGfdV′+∮φ∂n′GdS′.

These are the source and boundary contributions.

This is the core derivation.

To treat the singularity rigorously, remove a small ball around the observation point, integrate by parts, and shrink the ball; its boundary supplies the term u(r).

The image method is only a way to construct a suitable G, and images must lie outside the domain.

An unbounded domain also requires convergence and vanishing boundary terms at infinity.

Normals always point outward from the region being solved.

题目Problem

怎样构造三维上半空间中零边界、单位点源的 Dirichlet 格林函数？

How do we construct the Dirichlet Green function for a unit point source in the three-dimensional upper half-space with zero boundary values?

已知什么Given

区域是 y>0，真实源点为 r0=(x0,y0,z0) 且 y0>0。约定 ΔG=δ(r−r0)，并要求 G=0 于 y=0。

The domain is y>0, with real source r0=(x0,y0,z0) and y0>0. Use ΔG=δ(r−r0) and require G=0 on y=0.

要求什么Find

用镜像法写出 G，并逐项检查点源和边界。

Use the image method to write G, then check the point source and boundary term by term.

  1. 第 1 步Step 1放置镜像源Place the image source
    为什么这样做Why this step

    关于平面反射后，边界上任一点到两源的距离相等。

    After reflection across the plane, every boundary point is equally far from the two sources.

    具体怎么算Calculation

    把镜像点放在 r0*=(x0,−y0,z0)，并定义 R=|r−r0|、R*=|r−r0*|。

    Put the image at r0*=(x0,−y0,z0), and define R=|r−r0| and R*=|r−r0*|.

  2. 第 2 步Step 2写真实源项Write the real-source term
    为什么这样做Why this step

    本章采用 ΔG=+δ，所以三维单位点源前必须带负号。

    This chapter uses ΔG=+δ, so the three-dimensional unit-source term needs a minus sign.

    具体怎么算Calculation

    真实源贡献取 −1/(4πR)，它在 r=r0 产生单位正点源。

    Use −1/(4πR) for the real source; it produces a positive unit point source at r=r0.

  3. 第 3 步Step 3选择镜像符号Choose the image sign
    为什么这样做Why this step

    要让平面上的两个等距离项相消，镜像项必须与真实源项异号。

    The two equal-distance terms must have opposite signs to cancel on the plane.

    具体怎么算Calculation

    取 G=−1/(4πR)+1/(4πR*)；镜像点位于 y<0，所以第二项在求解区域 y>0 内没有奇点。

    Take G=−1/(4πR)+1/(4πR*). The image lies in y<0, so the second term has no singularity in the solution domain y>0.

  4. 第 4 步Step 4检查平面边界Check the plane boundary
    为什么这样做Why this step

    Dirichlet 格林函数最关键的附加条件是边界值为零。

    The defining extra condition for a Dirichlet Green function is a zero boundary value.

    具体怎么算Calculation

    当 y=0 时，R2=(x−x0)2+y02+(z−z0)2=R*2，于是 G=0。

    When y=0, R2=(x−x0)2+y02+(z−z0)2=R*2, so G=0.

  5. 第 5 步Step 5得到边界核Obtain the boundary kernel
    为什么这样做Why this step

    若边界值不是零，格林公式还需要 G 的外法向导数。

    For nonzero prescribed boundary data, Green's formula also needs the outward normal derivative of G.

    具体怎么算Calculation

    边界外法向为 −ey；求导后得到 P=y2π[(x−ξ)2+y2+(z−ζ)2]3/2，且 ∫R2Pdξdζ=1。

    The outward normal is −ey. Differentiation gives P=y2π[(x−ξ)2+y2+(z−ζ)2]3/2, with ∫R2Pdξdζ=1.

做完以后这样检查Check after solving

  • 域内只有 R=0 的真实奇点，R*=0 位于区域外。

    The only singularity in the domain is the real one at R=0; R*=0 lies outside.

  • 在 y=0 上有 R=R*，两个倒数项严格相消。

    On y=0, R=R*, so the two reciprocal-distance terms cancel exactly.

  • 边界核 P 为正且积分为 1，所以常边界值 C 会返回常数解 u=C。

    The kernel P is positive and integrates to 1, so constant boundary data C returns the constant solution u=C.

第一步先做总量检查。

再看为什么只差一个常数。

First check the total balance.

Integrate Δu=f over the domain and apply the divergence theorem: ∫VfdV=∮∂V∂nudS=∮∂VgdS.

The total source must equal total outward flux.

If the totals differ, no function can satisfy the problem, so further solving is unnecessary.

Next explain the arbitrary constant.

If u1,u2 have the same source and Neumann data, let w=u1−u2.

Then Δw=0,∂nw=0.

Integration by parts gives ∫V|∇w|2dV=∮w∂nwdS−∫VwΔwdV=0.

Thus ∇w=0, so w is constant in a connected domain.

The Green function must satisfy the same balance.

Instead use ΔGN=δ−1/|V|.

Its total source is 1−|V|/|V|=0, compatible with zero normal derivative.

The representation therefore determines the value relative to the mean.

Prescribing the mean or one point value fixes the absolute value.

This establishes the necessary balance and constant freedom; existence also relies on the relevant elliptic boundary-value theory.

If the difference satisfies ∂nw+αw=0, its energy identity is ∫|∇w|2=−∮αw2.

亥姆霍兹基本解与辐射条件Helmholtz fundamental solutions and radiation conditions

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

A single-frequency wave field must distinguish outgoing from incoming waves. Fix the time convention, then use the radiation condition to select the exponential sign.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

对空间简谐波，径向基本解是球面波除以距离。正负指数都满足同一个点源方程，区别来自辐射条件；判断出射和入射还必须说明时间因子。

For a time-harmonic wave, the radial fundamental solution is a spherical wave divided by distance. Both signs in the exponential satisfy the same point-source equation. The radiation condition selects one sign, and the time factor tells us which wave is outgoing.

(Δ+k2)G±=δ,G±(R)=−e±ikR4πR
使用条件When it applies

k>0，三维无界均匀空间。在时间因子 e−iωt 下，e+ikR/R 为出射波，满足 R(∂RG−ikG)→0；若时间因子改为 e+iωt，命名反转。

k>0 in uniform, unbounded three-dimensional space. With the time factor e−iωt, e+ikR/R is outgoing and satisfies R(∂RG−ikG)→0. If the time factor is e+iωt, the outgoing/incoming names are reversed.

展开分步证明 / 推导Open the step-by-step proof / derivation＋

设 R 是到点源的距离。

在 R>0 处，径向方程为 G′′+2G′/R+k2G=0。

令 v=RG，利用 (RG)′′/R=G′′+2G′/R，方程变成 v′′+k2v=0。

这个常系数方程给 v=AeikR+Be−ikR，因此 G=(AeikR+Be−ikR)/R。

只在源点外算微分方程不能决定 A,B；它们控制波的方向和点源强度。

在 R→0 展开得 G=(A+B)/R+O(1)。

外向小球通量趋于 −4π(A+B)，而 k2∫R<ϵGdV=O(ϵ2)→0，故单位点源要求 A+B=−1/(4π)。

分别取 B=0 或 A=0，就得到两种 G±=−e±ikR/(4πR)；结合源外方程，以挖球分部积分可得到完整分布等式。

选择出射还需远场条件。

在时间因子 e−iωt 下，正指数的相位 kR−ωt 随时间向更大的 R 传播，所以它为出射；对它求导得 ∂RG+−ikG+=eikR/(4πR2)，故 R(∂RG+−ikG+)→0。

负指数则不满足这一出射条件。

两种基本解之差为 G+−G−=−isin(kR)/(2πR)，在原点极限为 −ik/(2π)，是没有点源的齐次解。

Let R be distance from the source.

For R>0, the radial equation is G′′+2G′/R+k2G=0.

Set v=RG and use (RG)′′/R=G′′+2G′/R to reduce it to v′′+k2v=0.

The constant-coefficient equation gives v=AeikR+Be−ikR, hence G=(AeikR+Be−ikR)/R.

Checking the equation only away from the source cannot determine A,B; these coefficients control wave direction and source strength.

As R→0, expand to obtain G=(A+B)/R+O(1).

The small-sphere outward flux tends to −4π(A+B), while k2∫R<ϵGdV=O(ϵ2)→0.

A unit source therefore requires A+B=−1/(4π).

Choosing B=0 or A=0 gives G±=−e±ikR/(4πR).

Together with the source-free equation, integration by parts around a removed ball establishes the distributional equality.

Selecting an outgoing wave requires a far-field condition.

With time factor e−iωt, the phase kR−ωt moves toward larger R, so the positive exponent is outgoing.

Differentiation gives ∂RG+−ikG+=eikR/(4πR2), hence R(∂RG+−ikG+)→0.

The negative exponent fails this outgoing condition.

The difference is G+−G−=−isin(kR)/(2πR), whose origin limit is −ik/(2π); it is a homogeneous solution without a point source.

Different extra conditions thus select different fundamental solutions of the same equation.

Replacing the time factor by e+iωt reverses the incoming and outgoing labels.

推迟格林函数与因果响应Retarded Green functions and causal response

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

一个点发生的变化，要过一段传播时间才影响远处。把源项的时间向前回溯“距离除以速度”，就体现了因果关系。

A disturbance takes time to reach a distant point. Evaluate the source at an earlier time, delayed by distance divided by speed, to enforce causality.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

波动信息以有限速度传播：观察点当前的响应，来自源点在 R/c 之前的作用。推迟解要求响应不早于源的激发。

A wave travels at a finite speed. The response observed now comes from what the source did R/c earlier. A retarded solution is causal: it cannot respond before the source acts or before the signal arrives.

Gret=δ(t−t′−R/c)4πc2R,R=|r−r′|
使用条件When it applies

此处算符为 ∂t2−c2Δ，三维无界空间，零初始响应。若算符写成 c−2∂t2−Δ，格林函数相应乘 c2；初始位移、速度不为零时要另加齐次初值解。

Here the operator is ∂t2−c2Δ in unbounded three-dimensional space, with zero initial response. For the operator c−2∂t2−Δ, multiply the Green function by c2. Nonzero initial displacement or velocity requires an additional homogeneous initial-value solution.

展开分步证明 / 推导Open the step-by-step proof / derivation＋

以下是采用 Fourier 变换与分布恒等式的核心推导。

令 τ=t−t′，对空间使用 G=(2π)−3∫eik·RG^d3k 的逆变换约定。

算符 ∂t2−c2Δ 变为 G^ττ+c2|k|2G^=δ(τ)，即每个空间频率都是受瞬时单位冲击的振子。

因果性要求 τ<0 时为零。

跨过零时刻积分，导数必须跳跃1；为避免 δ′，函数本身连续。

因此 G^=H(τ)sin(cqτ)/(cq)，其中 q=|k|。

它在 τ=0+ 的值为0、导数为1，正好满足两个连接条件。

逆变换时以 R 为极轴，角积分为 4πsin(qR)/(qR)。

结合体积元 q2dq，对 R>0 得 G=H(τ)/(2π2cR)∫0∞sin(qR)sin(cqτ)dq；两个分母中的 q 正好抵掉体积元中的 q2。

使用分布恒等式 ∫0∞sin(qR)sin(cqτ)dq=π[δ(R−cτ)−δ(R+cτ)]/2。

当 R>0,τ≥0，第二项没有支撑；再用 δ(R−cτ)=δ(τ−R/c)/c，得到 Gret=δ(τ−R/c)/(4πc2R)。

此正弦积分不是通常意义的收敛积分；源点及传播前沿都按分布解释。

把格林函数与源叠加，时间积分用 δ 选出 t′=t−R/c，故零初始响应为 u(r,t)=∫f(r′,t−R/c)/(4πc2R)dV′，并按源的起始时刻限制积分。

若算符改为 c−2∂t2−Δ，整体响应应乘 c2；若初始位移或速度非零，另加齐次初值解。

The core derivation uses Fourier transforms and distribution identities.

Set τ=t−t′ and use the spatial inverse convention G=(2π)−3∫eik·RG^d3k.

The operator ∂t2−c2Δ becomes G^ττ+c2|k|2G^=δ(τ): each spatial frequency is an oscillator driven by a unit impulse.

Causality requires zero response for τ<0.

Integrating across zero makes the derivative jump by 1, while continuity avoids a δ′.

Thus G^=H(τ)sin(cqτ)/(cq), with q=|k|.

Its value at τ=0+ is 0 and its derivative is 1, satisfying both matching conditions.

In the inverse transform, choose the polar axis along R.

The angular integral is 4πsin(qR)/(qR).

Combining it with the measure q2dq, for R>0 we obtain G=H(τ)/(2π2cR)∫0∞sin(qR)sin(cqτ)dq.

The two denominator factors of q cancel the q2 in the measure.

Use the distribution identity ∫0∞sin(qR)sin(cqτ)dq=π[δ(R−cτ)−δ(R+cτ)]/2.

For R>0,τ≥0, the second term has no support.

With δ(R−cτ)=δ(τ−R/c)/c, this gives Gret=δ(τ−R/c)/(4πc2R).

The sine integral is not an ordinarily convergent integral; both the source point and wavefront are interpreted distributionally.

Superpose the Green function with the source.

The time delta selects t′=t−R/c, so the zero-initial-response solution is u(r,t)=∫f(r′,t−R/c)/(4πc2R)dV′, with the source start time respected.

For the operator c−2∂t2−Δ, multiply the response by c2.

Nonzero initial displacement or velocity requires an additional homogeneous initial-value solution.
