如果命题条件不足、展开区域包含奇点，或积分只能按主值理解，解析会明确说明。参考解答不是考试官方评分标准；遇到与课堂约定不同的符号或归一化时，优先统一约定再比较。Solutions flag missing assumptions, singularities in proposed expansion regions, and integrals requiring principal values. These references are not an official exam marking scheme. Align sign and normalization conventions with your course before comparing answers.

习题 1.3 · 6 →Exercise 1.3 · 6 →

原题未说明正则相交点条件；解析中补上。

The original question omits the regular-intersection condition. The solution supplies it.

习题 1.5 · 3 →Exercise 1.5 · 3 →

第(6)第二个指定区域 |z|>1 包含极点 z=2，原题不能按单个环域展开，须拆域。

In part (6), the second stated region |z|>1 contains the pole z=2. It cannot be treated as one annulus and must be split.

习题 1.6 · 3 →Exercise 1.6 · 3 →

第(5)无穷远不是孤立奇点，原题“包括无穷远点”在此不适用；应写出有限极点留数并说明无穷远留数无定义。

In part (5), infinity is not an isolated singularity, so the instruction “including infinity” does not apply there. List the finite-pole residues and state that the residue at infinity is undefined.

习题 1.7 · 1 →Exercise 1.7 · 1 →

第(4)(5)(7)原书未标PV，按普通反常积分理解均不收敛。解析先说明发散，再给对称挖孔定义的Cauchy主值。

The textbook does not label (4), (5), or (7) as PV. All three diverge as ordinary improper integrals. The solutions first state divergence, then give Cauchy principal values defined by symmetric exclusions.

习题 2.1 · 1 →Exercise 2.1 · 1 →

第二问的原函数含非零奇部分，正弦系数 b1=1。公开参考图片将所有 bn 写为零，漏掉了这一项。

The second function has a nonzero odd part, with sine coefficient b1=1. The public reference image labels every bn as zero and omits this term.

习题 2.1 · 3 →Exercise 2.1 · 3 →

在本站 a0/2 的 Fourier 级数记法下，a0=2l、bn=−2l/(nπ)。公开参考图片的正弦系数少了因子 2；常数项的记法不同需先统一。

With this site's Fourier-series convention a0/2, the coefficients are a0=2l and bn=−2l/(nπ). The public reference image misses a factor two in the sine coefficient. First align conventions for the constant term.

习题 2.1 · 4 →Exercise 2.1 · 4 →

端点条件用于选择延拓及本征基底。原函数 x 与规定端点值不相容时，级数在开区间或 L² 意义恢复原函数。

The endpoint conditions choose the extension and eigenfunction basis. When x is incompatible with the prescribed endpoint values, the series recovers the original function in the open interval or in the L² sense.

习题 2.1 · 5 →Exercise 2.1 · 5 →

原函数 x 的端点导数与指定的齐次条件不相容；不能把级数逐项求导后的端点值当成原函数的经典端点导数。

The endpoint derivatives of x are incompatible with the stated homogeneous conditions. Endpoint values of the termwise differentiated series cannot be treated as classical endpoint derivatives of the original function.

习题 2.1 · 6 →Exercise 2.1 · 6 →

初始函数与端点条件不相容时，按开区间或 L² 展开理解；端点及逐项求导需分别讨论。

If the initial function and endpoint conditions are incompatible, interpret the expansion on the open interval or in L². Discuss endpoint values and termwise differentiation separately.

习题 2.2 · 6 →Exercise 2.2 · 6 →

半轴题面未单独规定完整的变换规范；解析给出正弦变换及奇延拓的全轴变换。在 e−ikx 的约定下，全轴结果应为负的纯虚奇函数，公开参考图片的符号相反。

The half-line question does not fully specify its transform convention. The solution gives both the sine transform and the whole-line transform of the odd extension. Under e−ikx, the latter is a negative purely imaginary odd function; the public reference image has the opposite sign.

习题 2.2 · 7 →Exercise 2.2 · 7 →

在 f^=∫fe−ik·rd3r 的约定下，汤川势的变换为正 4π/(k2+α2)。公开参考图片的整体负号有误。

Under the convention f^=∫fe−ik·rd3r, the Yukawa-potential transform is positive 4π/(k2+α2). The public reference image has an incorrect overall minus sign.

习题 2.2 · 8 →Exercise 2.2 · 8 →

需用正则化或广义函数理解。相同 Fourier 约定下，结果为正 2π2/|k|，公开参考图片整体符号有误。

Regularization or distributions are required. With the same Fourier convention, the result is positive 2π2/|k|; the public reference image has an incorrect overall sign.

习题 2.2 · 9 →Exercise 2.2 · 9 →

Coulomb 势按广义变换理解，结果为正 4π/k2。公开参考图片整体符号有误；k=0 不能当普通函数值处理。

The Coulomb potential requires a generalized transform. The result is positive 4π/k2; the public reference image has an incorrect overall sign. k=0 is not an ordinary finite function value.

习题 2.4 · 4 →Exercise 2.4 · 4 →

L[ebtcoshat] 的分子是 p−b。公开参考图片将分子写为 (p−b)2，与直接积分和量纲检查均不符。

The numerator of L[ebtcoshat] is p−b. The public reference image gives (p−b)2, which disagrees with both direct integration and a dimensional check.

习题 3.2 · 4 →Exercise 3.2 · 4 →

教材题 4 与题 5 的题面重复。本站保留两个原题号，避免误认为遗漏；初值与右端绝热条件的角点不相容性在解析中说明。

Textbook questions 4 and 5 repeat the same problem. Both original numbers are retained to avoid appearing to omit a question. The solution explains the initial–boundary incompatibility at the right endpoint.

习题 3.2 · 5 →Exercise 3.2 · 5 →

此题与教材同节题 4 重复，保留原题号；本征展开与初边值相容性同题 4。

This repeats question 4 in the same section. The original number is retained; the eigenfunction expansion and compatibility discussion are the same.

习题 3.4 · 4 →Exercise 3.4 · 4 →

第二问对任意实 Robin 边界系数声称本征值非负不成立。解析给出 λ=−1 的反例，并列出可保证非负的能量条件。

Part (2) incorrectly claims nonnegative eigenvalues for arbitrary real Robin coefficients. The solution gives a λ=−1 counterexample and sufficient energy conditions for nonnegativity.

习题 4.1 · 2 →Exercise 4.1 · 2 →

对任意可微函数，W≡0 未必推出整个区间内线性相关；需要同一正规齐次方程解等额外条件。解析给出反例与正确证明。

For arbitrary differentiable functions, W≡0 need not imply linear dependence on the whole interval. An extra assumption, such as solving the same regular homogeneous equation, is needed. The solution gives both a counterexample and the corrected proof.

习题 4.1 · 3 →Exercise 4.1 · 3 →

原命题缺少 q=p′ 条件。一般 Abel 公式为 W=Cexp(−∫q/pdx)；只有自伴形式才有 W=C/p。

The original statement lacks the condition q=p′. The general Abel formula is W=Cexp(−∫q/pdx). Only the self-adjoint form gives W=C/p.

习题 4.2 · 5 →Exercise 4.2 · 5 →

公开参考图片未给完整的第二支解，且递推式需包含上一项系数。解析补出重根 Frobenius 的对数支及降阶积分。

The public reference image does not provide the complete second solution, and its recurrence must include the preceding coefficient. The solution supplies the logarithmic Frobenius solution and the reduction-of-order integral.

习题 4.2 · 6 →Exercise 4.2 · 6 →

题设最高次项为 (−x)n，与常用 Laguerre 多项式相差 n!。这是归一化约定差异，不能混用。

The required leading term (−x)n differs from the common Laguerre convention by a factor n!. These are different normalizations and must not be mixed.

习题 5.2 · 7 →Exercise 5.2 · 7 →

原题未给无穷远条件；解析采用衰减条件，并讨论 Robin 系数 h=R/(n+1) 时的共振、无解或不唯一。

The original problem omits an infinity condition. This solution assumes decay and discusses resonance, nonexistence, and nonuniqueness when the Robin parameter is h=R/(n+1).

习题 5.3 · 3 →Exercise 5.3 · 3 →

自由冷却边界须保留表面换热参数。题面未给具体数值时，用 Robin 参数表示本征方程，不能自行按零温边界替代。

A free-cooling boundary requires the surface heat-transfer parameter. If its value is not given, retain the Robin parameter in the eigenvalue equation instead of replacing the condition by zero temperature.

习题 6.1 · 1 →Exercise 6.1 · 1 →

无界一维问题未给唯一性条件。标准基本解只是一个规范选择，不能额外强加两端同时衰减。

The whole-line problem does not specify uniqueness conditions. The standard fundamental solution is a normalization choice; imposing decay at both ends would be impossible.

习题 6.2 · 1 →Exercise 6.2 · 1 →

原题只写平面边界。无界域还需指定可接受的远场行为与积分收敛条件，否则解不唯一。

The problem specifies only the plane boundary. An unbounded domain also needs acceptable far-field behavior and convergent integrals; otherwise the solution is not unique.

习题 6.2 · 3 →Exercise 6.2 · 3 →

例 5.2.2 的式 (5.2.97) 点源符号与其正电荷 Coulomb 电势不一致。比较时应采用物理约定 ΔΦ=−ρe/ε0，同时保留本章 ΔG=δ 的约定。

The point-source sign in Example 5.2.2, Eq. (5.2.97), is inconsistent with its positive-charge Coulomb potential. Use the physical convention ΔΦ=−ρe/ε0 when comparing, while retaining this chapter's convention ΔG=δ.
