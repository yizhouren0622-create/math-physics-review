傅里叶级数：为什么这样做Fourier series: why the method works

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

一条周期曲线可以拆成许多简单的正弦和余弦。积分得到的系数，就在回答“每一种波要放多少”。先确认周期和奇偶性。

A periodic curve can be built from sine and cosine waves. Each coefficient says how much of one wave is needed. Identify the period and parity before integrating.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

把一个周期内的函数投影到正交的正弦、余弦上；每个系数衡量对应频率的成分。端点条件决定用正弦、余弦还是半整数频率。

Project one period of a function onto orthogonal sines and cosines. Each coefficient measures the amount of one frequency. Endpoint conditions tell us whether to use sine, cosine, or half-integer frequencies.

f(x)~a02+∑n=1∞(ancosnπxl+bnsinnπxl)
使用条件When it applies

周期为 2l；分段光滑时，连续点收敛到函数值，跳跃点收敛到左右极限平均值。

The period is 2l. For a piecewise smooth function, the series converges to the function at continuity points and to the average of the one-sided limits at jumps.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

两边乘 cos(mπx/l)，在 [−l,l] 积分。不同频率的积分为零，同频余弦平方积分为 l，因此 am=l−1∫−llf(x)cos(mπx/l)dx。同理得到 bm；常数项用积分得到 a0。

Multiply both sides by cos(mπx/l) and integrate over [−l,l]. Different frequencies integrate to zero. The squared cosine at the same frequency integrates to l, giving am=l−1∫−llf(x)cos(mπx/l)dx. Similarly we obtain bm; integration of the constant term gives a0.

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

将 f(x)=x 在 (−π,π) 展开。

Expand f(x)=x on (−π,π).

解答

Solution

奇函数只剩正弦项。bn=2π∫0πxsinnxdx=2(−1)n+1/n，故 x=2∑n≥1(−1)n+1sinnx/n，端点周期延拓处和为 0。

The function is odd, so only sine terms remain. Since bn=2π∫0πxsinnxdx=2(−1)n+1/n, we obtain x=2∑n≥1(−1)n+1sinnx/n. At the endpoints of the periodic extension, the sum is 0.

傅里叶积分变换：为什么这样做Fourier transforms: why the method works

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

不周期的函数也能拆成频率成分，只是频率变成连续的。先抄清正、逆变换的符号与系数，再代入积分。

A nonperiodic function can also be resolved into frequencies, now continuous ones. Write the forward and inverse conventions before calculating an integral.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

傅里叶变换把全轴函数分解为连续频率。微分在频域变成乘 ik，局部窄脉冲对应宽频谱。本章统一正变换不带系数、逆变换带 1/(2π)。

The Fourier transform splits a whole-line function into a continuous range of frequencies. Differentiation becomes multiplication by ik in frequency space. A narrow pulse has a broad spectrum. Throughout this chapter, the forward transform has no prefactor and the inverse has 1/(2π).

F(k)=∫−∞∞f(x)e−ikxdx,f(x)=12π∫−∞∞F(k)eikxdk
使用条件When it applies

通常要求可积或平方可积；阶跃、符号函数和库仑势需按广义函数或正则化理解。

Usually require integrability or square integrability. Step functions, the sign function, and Coulomb potentials require distributions or regularization.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

由分部积分，∫f′(x)e−ikxdx=[fe−ikx]−∞∞+ik∫fe−ikxdx。边界项消失时 F[f′]=ikF。由定义换元还得 F[f(ax)]=|a|−1F(k/a)。

Integration by parts gives ∫f′(x)e−ikxdx=[fe−ikx]−∞∞+ik∫fe−ikxdx. When the boundary term vanishes, F[f′]=ikF. A change of variable in the definition also gives F[f(ax)]=|a|−1F(k/a).

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

求 f(x)=e−a|x| 的变换，a>0。

Find the transform of f(x)=e−a|x| for a>0.

解答

Solution

利用偶性：F=2∫0∞e−axcoskxdx=2Re(a−ik)−1=2a/(a2+k2)。

Use evenness: F=2∫0∞e−axcoskxdx=2Re(a−ik)−1=2a/(a2+k2).

记忆线索Memory cue

正变换负指数，逆变换正指数；乘积与卷积的 2π 别丢。

Use a negative exponential for the forward transform and a positive one for the inverse. Keep track of 2π in product and convolution formulas.

Fourier 变换的平移、微分与卷积Shifts, derivatives, and convolution in Fourier transforms

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

平移、求导、卷积各有对应的变换规则，可以省掉长积分。先辨认题目做了哪一种操作，再核对符号和系数。

Translation, differentiation, and convolution have transform rules that save integration. Identify the operation first, then check its sign and normalization.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

变换把空间微分变成代数乘法，把卷积变成乘积。使用性质前，先确认指数正负号和 2π 放在哪一侧。

The transform turns spatial derivatives into algebraic multiplication and convolutions into products. Before using a property, check the exponential sign and which side contains 2π.

f′^=ikF,f(x−a)^=e−ikaF,f*g^=FG,fg^=F*G2π
使用条件When it applies

规范为 F(k)=∫f(x)e−ikxdx、f(x)=(2π)−1∫F(k)eikxdk。普通函数需有足够可积性、可换序且分部积分端点消失；分布情形按定义推广。

The convention is F(k)=∫f(x)e−ikxdx and f(x)=(2π)−1∫F(k)eikxdk. For ordinary functions, require sufficient integrability, justified interchange of integrals, and vanishing integration-by-parts boundary terms. Distributional versions follow by definition.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

微分性质对 ∫f′e−ikxdx 分部积分得到；平移令 y=x−a；卷积写成二重积分后令 y=x−s，即分离为 F(k)G(k)。乘积性质将 f 的逆变换代入，再换序得到 1/(2π) 倍卷积。

For the derivative property, integrate ∫f′e−ikxdx by parts. For a shift, set y=x−a. For convolution, write the double integral and set y=x−s; it separates into F(k)G(k). For the product property, substitute the inverse transform of f and interchange integrals to obtain 1/(2π) times the convolution.

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

已知 e−x2^=πe−k2/4，求 e−(x−a)2 的变换。

Given e−x2^=πe−k2/4, find the transform of e−(x−a)2.

解答

Solution

这是平移 a，故结果为 e−ikaπe−k2/4；模不变而相位改变。

This is a shift by a, giving e−ikaπe−k2/4. The modulus is unchanged; the phase changes.

记忆线索Memory cue

空间平移加相位；微分乘 ik；卷积变乘积。

A spatial shift adds phase. A derivative multiplies by ik. A convolution becomes a product.

δ 函数与抽样性质The δ distribution and sampling

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

δ不是普通的尖峰函数，而是“在指定位置取值”的积分规则。遇到缩放先处理绝对值系数，遇到导数先处理负号。

Delta is an integration rule that samples a value at one point. For scaling, handle the absolute-value factor; for a derivative, handle the minus sign.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

δ 应看作作用在测试函数上的抽样规则，不能当作普通的“某点等于无穷大”的函数进行逐点运算。单位面积且不断变窄的脉冲可以逼近它。

Think of δ as a sampling rule acting on test functions. It is not an ordinary function that can be manipulated pointwise as “infinite at one point.” Pulses with unit area and shrinking width can approach it.

∫δ(x−a)φ(x)dx=φ(a),δ(g(x))=∑g(xj)=0δ(x−xj)|g′(xj)|
使用条件When it applies

后一公式要求根为单根；积分区间须包含相应根。

The second formula requires simple roots. The integration interval must contain the relevant roots.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

在每个单根附近令 u=g(x)，换元时 dx=du/|g′(xj)|，再利用 δ(u) 的抽样性质。多个根的贡献相加。

Near each simple root, set u=g(x). The change of variable gives dx=du/|g′(xj)|. Apply the sampling property of δ(u), then add the contributions from all roots.

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

计算 ∫−22(1+x2)δ(x2−1)dx。

Evaluate ∫−22(1+x2)δ(x2−1)dx.

解答

Solution

根为 ±1，导数绝对值均为 2。因此 δ(x2−1)=[δ(x−1)+δ(x+1)]/2，积分为 (2+2)/2=2。

The roots are ±1 and both derivative magnitudes are 2. Thus δ(x2−1)=[δ(x−1)+δ(x+1)]/2, and the integral is (2+2)/2=2.

记忆线索Memory cue

找根、除导数绝对值、再抽样。

Find the roots → divide by the absolute derivative → sample the remaining function.

拉普拉斯变换：为什么这样做Laplace transforms: why the method works

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

拉普拉斯变换把时间函数换成关于s的函数。先把题目拆成已知变换表里的部分，再用平移或微分规则。

The Laplace transform turns a time function into a function of s. Break the expression into table entries, then apply shift or derivative rules as needed.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

拉普拉斯变换只看 t≥0，指数衰减因子使更多函数可变换，尤其适合初值问题。导数的变换会自动带入初值。

The Laplace transform uses only t≥0. Its decaying exponential allows a larger class of functions to be transformed and is especially useful for initial-value problems. Transforming a derivative automatically includes the initial values.

F(p)=∫0∞f(t)e−ptdt,L[f′]=pF−f(0+)
使用条件When it applies

f 分段连续且为指数阶；Rep 大于其增长率。

f is piecewise continuous and of exponential order. Rep must exceed its growth rate.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

分部积分：L[f′]=[fe−pt]0∞+p∫0∞fe−ptdt=pF−f(0+)。再用一次得 L[f′′]=p2F−pf(0+)−f′(0+)。

Integration by parts gives L[f′]=[fe−pt]0∞+p∫0∞fe−ptdt=pF−f(0+). Applying it again gives L[f′′]=p2F−pf(0+)−f′(0+).

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

求 L[te2t]。

Find L[te2t].

解答

Solution

从 L[e2t]=1/(p−2) 出发，利用 L[tf]=−F′(p)，结果为 1/(p−2)2，收敛域 Rep>2。

Start from L[e2t]=1/(p−2) and apply L[tf]=−F′(p). The result is 1/(p−2)2 with convergence region Rep>2.

记忆线索Memory cue

时间乘 t，频域负求导；乘指数，频域平移。

Multiplication by t in time becomes a negative frequency derivative. Multiplication by an exponential shifts the frequency variable.

拉普拉斯变换解常微分方程：为什么这样做Laplace solutions of ODEs: why the method works

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

变换能把微分方程改写成代数方程，初值也一起进入。按“变换→解代数式→拆分→逆变换”四步做，初值不要漏。

Transform the differential equation, solve the algebraic equation, decompose it, and invert. Initial values enter the derivative transforms, so include them at the first step.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

把微分方程连同初值一次性变成代数方程，解出像函数后做部分分式、移位或卷积逆变换。

Transform the differential equation and initial values together into an algebraic equation. Solve for the transformed function, then invert using partial fractions, shifts, or convolution.

y′′+ay′+by=f⟹(p2+ap+b)Y=F+py(0)+y′(0)+ay(0)
使用条件When it applies

初值取 0+；分段外力可以用阶跃函数编码。

Take the initial values at 0+. Encode piecewise forcing with step functions.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

分别代入 L[y′]=pY−y(0) 和 L[y′′]=p2Y−py(0)−y′(0)，整理所有含 Y 的项即可。逆变换线性性保证得到原方程的解。

Substitute L[y′]=pY−y(0) and L[y′′]=p2Y−py(0)−y′(0), then collect every term containing Y. Linearity of the inverse transform recovers a solution of the original equation.

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

解 y′+y=1, y(0)=0。

Solve y′+y=1, y(0)=0.

解答

Solution

(p+1)Y=1/p，故 Y=1/p−1/(p+1)，得到 y=1−e−t。代回 y′+y=1 且初值为零。

We have (p+1)Y=1/p, hence Y=1/p−1/(p+1) and y=1−e−t. Substitution gives y′+y=1, with zero initial value.

记忆线索Memory cue

先写导数变换和初值，再解 Y，最后代回核验。

Write derivative transforms and initial values first → solve for Y → substitute back to check.

恒等式与泊松核展开Identities and a Poisson-kernel expansion

将下列周期函数展开为傅里叶级数：(1) f(x)=cos2x；(2) f(x)=1/[1+12sinx]。

Expand these periodic functions in Fourier series: (1) f(x)=cos2x; (2) f(x)=1/[1+12sinx].

这道题要你做什么What are you trying to find?

用恒等式或几何级数，避免硬算一长串系数积分。

看到什么，就想到这个方法Clue → choice of method
