波动问题：方程与边界一起看Wave problems: read the equation and boundary conditions together

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

弦的运动既受方程控制，也受两端怎样固定控制。先列初始形状、初始速度和端点条件，再选适合的正弦或余弦。

A vibrating string is controlled by both its equation and its endpoints. List initial shape, initial velocity, and endpoint conditions before choosing sine or cosine modes.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

波动方程来自“质量×加速度＝受力”。弦横振动波速为 c=T/ρ，杆纵振动波速为 c=Y/ρ。固定端给位移条件，自由端给应力或斜率条件。

The wave equation comes from “mass × acceleration = force.” A string’s transverse wave speed is c=T/ρ; a rod’s longitudinal wave speed is c=Y/ρ. A fixed end prescribes displacement. A free end prescribes stress or slope.

utt=c2uxx,u=∑n(Ancosωnt+Bnsinωnt)Xn(x)
使用条件When it applies

线性小振幅、均匀材料；每个边界类型要选择对应的本征函数。

Small linear oscillations and a uniform material. Choose spatial eigenfunctions matching each boundary type.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

令 u=XT，分离为 X″+λX=0、T″+c2λT=0。固定两端使 Xn=sin(nπx/l)，ωn=cnπ/l。用初始位移投影给 An，初速度投影除以 ωn 给 Bn。

Set u=XT and separate to obtain X″+λX=0 and T″+c2λT=0. Two fixed ends give Xn=sin(nπx/l) and ωn=cnπ/l. Project the initial displacement to obtain An. Project the initial velocity and divide by ωn to obtain Bn.

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

两端固定弦的初始位移为 sin(πx/l)，初速度零，求振动。

A string fixed at both ends has initial displacement sin(πx/l) and zero initial velocity. Find its motion.

解答

Solution

只有第一模态：u(x,t)=sin(πx/l)cos(cπt/l)。代回 utt=c2uxx，端点及两初值均成立。

Only the first mode is present: u(x,t)=sin(πx/l)cos(cπt/l). It satisfies utt=c2uxx, both end conditions, and both initial conditions.

记忆线索Memory cue

位移决定余弦时间项，速度决定正弦时间项；边界先于系数。

Displacement supplies cosine-in-time terms; velocity supplies sine-in-time terms. Choose the boundary-compatible modes before finding coefficients.

达朗贝尔公式与特征线D’Alembert’s formula and characteristics

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

无限长弦的初始形状会拆成向左、向右传播的两部分。看自变量里的加减号判断方向，再用初值确定它们的大小。

An infinite string’s initial shape splits into left- and right-traveling pieces. Read the signs in their arguments to determine direction, then use the initial data to determine amplitudes.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

无限弦的位移由两个反向传播的波叠加。初速度不是直接乘 t，而是在影响区间上积分。固定端半轴问题可以先奇延拓。

An infinite string’s displacement is the sum of waves traveling in opposite directions. Initial velocity contributes an integral over the region of influence, rather than simply velocity multiplied by t. A fixed-end half-line can be handled by odd extension.

u(x,t)=φ(x−ct)+φ(x+ct)2+12c∫x−ctx+ctv(s)ds
使用条件When it applies

utt=c2uxx，全轴、无外力；初值为位移 φ 和速度 v。经典解要求足够光滑。半轴固定端用奇延拓，绝热/自由端用偶延拓。

For utt=c2uxx on the full line, without forcing, φ is the initial displacement and v the initial velocity. A classical solution needs sufficient smoothness. On a half-line, use odd extension for a fixed end and even extension for an insulated/free end.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

特征变量 ξ=x−ct、η=x+ct 令方程化为 uξη=0，故 u=F(ξ)+H(η)。t=0 时 F+H=φ、c(H′−F′)=v，积分解出两函数并相加，得到所列公式。对 t 求导并令 t=0 可直接核验 v。

With characteristic variables ξ=x−ct and η=x+ct, the equation becomes uξη=0, so u=F(ξ)+H(η). At t=0, F+H=φ and c(H′−F′)=v. Integrate to find the two functions and add them, giving the displayed formula. Differentiating in t at t=0 directly verifies the initial velocity v.

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

全轴初位移为 0、初速度为常数 V，位移如何变化？

On the full line, the initial displacement is zero and the initial velocity is a constant V. How does displacement change?

解答

Solution

u=(2c)−1∫x−ctx+ctVds=Vt。空间上均匀运动，所以 uxx=utt=0，初速度正确。

u=(2c)−1∫x−ctx+ctVds=Vt. The motion is spatially uniform, so uxx=utt=0, and the initial velocity is correct.

记忆线索Memory cue

位移取两端平均；速度取中间面积除 2c。

Average the displacement at the two endpoints; integrate the velocity between them and divide by 2c.

非齐次方程的模态方程与共振Modal equations for forcing and resonance

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

外力会不断向系统输入运动。先把空间形状拆成模态，每个模态只剩一条时间方程；外力频率相同时要单独处理共振。

A force drives the motion over time. Expand its spatial shape into modes, solve a time equation for each, and treat resonance separately when frequencies coincide.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

空间边界齐次后，把外力和初值投影到相同本征函数上。每个模态都是一个受迫振子，共振时不能使用零分母的非共振公式。

Once spatial boundary conditions are homogeneous, project the forcing and initial data onto the same eigenfunctions. Each mode is a forced oscillator. At resonance, the nonresonant formula has a zero denominator and must be replaced.

qn′′+ωn2qn=Fn(t),qn=Ancosωnt+Bnsinωnt+1ωn∫0tFn(s)sin[ωn(t−s)]ds
使用条件When it applies

采用正交空间本征系，ωn>0；Bn=qn′(0)/ωn。零模态须单独解 q0′′=F0。阻尼项存在时要相应修改时间方程。

Use an orthogonal spatial eigenbasis with ωn>0 and Bn=qn′(0)/ωn. A zero mode must separately solve q0′′=F0. Damping modifies the time equation.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

对空间本征展开代入方程并利用正交性，得到各 qₙ 方程。积分核 sinωn(t−s)/ωn 在 t=s 时为零、t 导数为 1，所以二次求导后产生 Fₙ(t)，且卷积项的两条初值均为零。

Insert the spatial expansion into the equation and use orthogonality to obtain the equations for qₙ. The kernel sinωn(t−s)/ωn is zero at t=s and has t derivative 1 there. Differentiating the convolution twice therefore produces Fₙ(t), while both initial values of the convolution are zero.

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

零初值，q′′+ω2q=Fcosωt 的共振响应是什么？

With zero initial data, what is the resonant response of q′′+ω2q=Fcosωt?

解答

Solution

代入卷积或待定系数法得 q=Ftsinωt/(2ω)。包络随 t 增长。直接求两次导数可核验强迫与零初值。

The convolution or undetermined coefficients gives q=Ftsinωt/(2ω). Its envelope grows with t. Differentiating twice verifies both the forcing and the zero initial data.

记忆线索Memory cue

空间先正交投影，时间再解振子；遇零分母先检查共振。

Project in space first, then solve the oscillator in time. A zero denominator means check resonance.

输运问题：方程与边界一起看Transport problems: read the equation and boundary conditions together

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

热会让不均匀的温度逐渐变平。先看端点是固定温度还是绝热，这决定展开的基底；再给每一项乘衰减因子。

Diffusion smooths uneven temperatures. Fixed-temperature and insulated endpoints select different bases. Attach the appropriate decay factor to each mode.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

热量守恒与傅里叶导热定律给热方程。无源热方程的各非零空间模态随时间指数衰减；保温边界保留零模态，固定零温边界没有零模态。

Heat conservation and Fourier’s law give the heat equation. Every nonzero spatial mode of the source-free heat equation decays exponentially. Insulated boundaries retain a zero mode; fixed zero-temperature boundaries do not.

ut=κΔu+s,κ=kρc
使用条件When it applies

均匀介质，k 为导热系数、ρc 为单位体积热容；外源 s 已除以热容。

The medium is uniform. k is thermal conductivity and ρc is heat capacity per unit volume. The source s has already been divided by that heat capacity.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

一维小段 dx 的内能增加率为 ρcSutdx；两端净输入热率为 kS[ux(x+dx)−ux(x)]。除以 Sdx 并取极限得 ρcut=kuxx+q。分离变量使时间因子满足 T′+κλT=0，所以 T=e−κλt。

For a segment dx, the rate of increase of internal energy is ρcSutdx. Net heat input through its ends is kS[ux(x+dx)−ux(x)]. Divide by Sdx and take the limit: ρcut=kuxx+q. Separation gives T′+κλT=0, hence T=e−κλt.

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

两端零温杆初温 sin(2πx/l)，求温度。

A rod held at zero temperature at both ends starts at sin(2πx/l). Find its temperature.

解答

Solution

u=e−4κπ2t/l2sin(2πx/l)。空间二阶导数带来 −(2π/l)2，与时间衰减率相同。

u=e−4κπ2t/l2sin(2πx/l). Two spatial derivatives contribute −(2π/l)2, matching the time decay rate.

记忆线索Memory cue

热方程时间一阶，初值只要一个；频率越高衰减越快。

The heat equation is first order in time and needs one initial condition. Higher spatial frequencies decay faster.

热核、镜像延拓与扩散尺度The heat kernel, image extension, and diffusion scale

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

热核告诉你一小团热经过一段时间会扩散成什么样。把各处初温加权叠加，就得到以后温度；有边界时要用合适的镜像。

The heat kernel describes how a concentrated pulse spreads. Weight and add these responses using the initial temperature, with suitable images when a boundary is present.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

无限域热核是随时间变宽的 Gaussian。半轴零温用反号镜像，半轴绝热用同号镜像。扩散影响尺度随 t 增长。

The infinite-domain heat kernel is a Gaussian that broadens with time. For a zero-temperature half-line boundary, subtract the reflected image. For an insulated boundary, add it. The diffusion scale grows as t.

K(x,t)=e−x2/(4κt)4πκt,u(x,t)=∫RK(x−ξ,t)φ(ξ)dξ
使用条件When it applies

ut=κuxx、κ>0、t>0、无源全轴。初值按连续点极限或适当 Lᵖ 意义恢复。半轴边界必须先区分 Dirichlet 和 Neumann。

For ut=κuxx, κ>0 and t>0, on the source-free full line. Initial data are recovered at continuity points or in an appropriate Lᵖ sense. On the half-line, distinguish Dirichlet from Neumann boundaries first.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

Fourier 变换给 u^t=−κk2u^，故 u^=e−κk2tφ^。Gaussian 逆变换给 K，且 ∫Kdx=1。奇延拓在半轴化为 K(x−ξ)−K(x+ξ)，使 x=0 的值抵消；偶延拓化为加号，使 x=0 的导数抵消。

The Fourier transform gives u^t=−κk2u^, hence u^=e−κk2tφ^. The inverse Gaussian transform gives K, and ∫Kdx=1. Odd extension becomes K(x−ξ)−K(x+ξ) on the half-line, canceling the value at x=0. Even extension uses a plus sign and cancels the derivative at x=0.

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

初温为单位点脉冲，其总热量随时间改变吗？

For a unit point pulse of initial temperature, does the total heat change with time?

解答

Solution

温度为 K，总热量 ∫Kdx=1 保持不变；峰值按 t−1/2 降低，宽度按 κt 增大。

The temperature is K and its integral ∫Kdx=1 stays constant. The peak decreases as t−1/2, while the width increases as κt.

记忆线索Memory cue

热核总量为 1；零值边界相减，零导数边界相加。

The heat kernel has total mass 1. Subtract images for a zero value; add them for a zero derivative.

非齐次边界先齐次化Make nonhomogeneous boundaries homogeneous first

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

端点不为零时，直接套零边界的正弦展开会失败。先减去一个满足边界的简单函数，让剩下的未知量在端点为零。

For nonzero boundary data, subtract a simple function that already satisfies the boundary. The remainder then has zero boundary values and can use the standard expansion.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

直接分离变量要求齐次边界。先找一个满足边界的简单函数 h，再令 u=v+h。若边界随时间变，v 的方程通常出现新的源项。

Direct separation of variables needs homogeneous boundaries. First choose a simple function h satisfying the prescribed boundary values, then set u=v+h. If the boundary changes with time, the equation for v usually acquires a new source.

u=v+h,vt−κvxx=f−(ht−κhxx)
使用条件When it applies

例如 u(0,t)=A(t)、u(L,t)=B(t) 可选 h=A+(B−A)x/L。v 的边界为零，但初值必须同时减 h(x,0)。

For example, with u(0,t)=A(t) and u(L,t)=B(t), choose h=A+(B−A)x/L. The boundary values of v are zero, but its initial data must also have h(x,0) subtracted.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

把 u=v+h 逐项代入原方程，移项即可。由于 h 满足原边界，v 满足齐次边界。常值边界的线性 h 有 ht=hxx=0，不新增源；时变边界保留 −ht，否则代回原方程会有残差。

Substitute u=v+h term by term and move the h terms to the right. Since h satisfies the original boundaries, v has homogeneous boundaries. A linear h for constant boundary values has ht=hxx=0 and adds no source. For time-dependent boundaries, retain −ht; omitting it leaves a residual in the original equation.

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

两端保持 0 和 T，初温为 0 的细杆，稳态是什么？

A thin rod starts at zero temperature and its ends are held at 0 and T. What is its steady state?

解答

Solution

h=Tx/L。令 v=u−h，v 两端零值、初值 −Tx/L，可作正弦展开并逐模态衰减。t→∞ 时 v→0，所以 u→Tx/L。

Take h=Tx/L. Then v=u−h has zero values at both ends and initial data −Tx/L. Expand v in sine modes, each of which decays. As t→∞, v→0, so u→Tx/L.

记忆线索Memory cue

边界先减掉；初值也减掉；方程检查是否新增源。

Subtract the boundary function. Subtract it from the initial data too. Check whether the equation gains a source.

稳定场问题：方程与边界一起看Steady fields: read the equation and boundaries together

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

稳定场不再随时间变化。先找区域形状，再让分离出的每个因子满足对应边界；最后用边界上的函数确定系数。

A steady field no longer changes with time. Use the region’s geometry to separate variables, enforce the boundary conditions, and determine coefficients from the boundary data.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

稳态意味着时间不变，无源场满足拉普拉斯方程。矩形区域分离后，一个方向是三角函数，另一个方向是双曲函数；非齐次边界通过投影确定系数。

A steady field does not depend on time. A source-free steady field satisfies Laplace’s equation. On a rectangle, separation gives trigonometric functions in one direction and hyperbolic functions in the other. Project the nonzero boundary values to find coefficients.

Δu=0,X″+λX=0,Y″−λY=0
使用条件When it applies

无源矩形区域；用齐次边界所在方向决定正交基，再满足非齐次边界。

A source-free rectangular region. Choose the orthogonal basis using the homogeneous boundaries, then satisfy the nonhomogeneous boundary.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

将 u=XY 代入 uxx+uyy=0，得到 X″/X=−Y″/Y=−λ。如果 x=0,a 为零值，则 λ=(nπ/a)2、X=sin(nπx/a)。若 y=0 也为零值，Y 取 sinh(nπy/a)。

Substituting u=XY into uxx+uyy=0 gives X″/X=−Y″/Y=−λ. If the values at x=0,a are zero, λ=(nπ/a)2 and X=sin(nπx/a). If the value at y=0 is also zero, choose Y as sinh(nπy/a).

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

矩形上仅顶边给 u(x,b)=sin(πx/a)，其余三边为零。

On a rectangle, the top edge has u(x,b)=sin(πx/a) and the other three edges have zero values.

解答

Solution

u=sin(πx/a)sinh(πy/a)/sinh(πb/a)；代入拉普拉斯方程，三零边和顶边同时满足。

u=sin(πx/a)sinh(πy/a)/sinh(πb/a). It satisfies Laplace’s equation, all three zero edges, and the prescribed top edge.

记忆线索Memory cue

先用齐次两边选正交基，再用双曲函数跨到非零边。

Use two homogeneous sides to choose the orthogonal basis, then a hyperbolic factor to reach the nonzero side.

Laplace 方程的唯一性与最大值原理Uniqueness and the maximum principle for Laplace’s equation

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

算出一个解以后，还要知道是否可能有另一个解。比较两个解的差，并结合边界条件，能证明差只能为零。

After finding a solution, ask whether another one could exist. Subtract two candidate solutions and use their zero difference on the boundary to prove uniqueness.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

边界值不仅用来定系数，也决定解是否唯一。判断两种方法的结果是否相同，可以研究两解之差。

Boundary values do more than fix coefficients: they determine whether the solution is unique. To compare results from two methods, study their difference.

Δw=0,w|∂V=0⟹w≡0
使用条件When it applies

有界连通域、足够正则的调和函数及 Dirichlet 边界。外域必须给无穷远条件。纯 Neumann 问题只确定到常数，且要有相容条件。

A bounded connected domain, sufficiently regular harmonic functions, and Dirichlet boundaries. An exterior domain also requires behavior at infinity. A pure Neumann problem determines the solution only up to a constant and requires compatibility.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

两解之差 w 满足齐次方程和零边界。Green 第一公式给 ∫V|∇w|2dV=∮w∂nw−∫wΔw=0，故 w 为常数，零边界再定为零。最大值原理也给上下界都为零。

The difference w of two solutions is harmonic with zero boundary values. Green’s first identity gives ∫V|∇w|2dV=∮w∂nw−∫wΔw=0. Thus w is constant; its zero boundary value makes it zero. The maximum principle also bounds w above and below by zero.

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

球面温度恒为 5，球内无热源，温度是否可能在中心更高？

A sphere’s surface is held at temperature 5 and there is no internal heat source. Could the center be hotter?

解答

Solution

常函数 5 已满足方程和边界，由唯一性只能为 5。最大值原理也排除内部超过边界最大值的稳态。

The constant function 5 satisfies the equation and the boundary. Uniqueness makes it the only solution. The maximum principle also prevents a steady interior value above the boundary maximum.

记忆线索Memory cue

两解相减；能量为零；边界定常数。

Subtract two solutions. Zero energy implies a constant. Use the boundary to fix that constant.

施图姆–刘维尔本征值问题：方程与边界一起看Sturm–Liouville problems: read the equation and boundaries together

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

只有某些参数能让非零函数同时满足方程和两端边界，这些参数叫本征值。先分正、零、负三种情况，不要默认只有正值。

An eigenvalue is a parameter allowing a nonzero solution to satisfy the equation and both boundaries. Examine positive, zero, and negative cases separately.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

不同边界下看似不同的级数，统一来自自伴本征值问题。权函数决定投影内积。实本征值依赖自伴边界；本征值非负还需要能量二次型非负，不能只看微分方程系数。

The different series arising from different boundaries all come from self-adjoint eigenvalue problems. The weight function defines the projection inner product. Self-adjoint boundaries ensure real eigenvalues. Nonnegative eigenvalues additionally require a nonnegative energy quadratic form; the differential-equation coefficients alone do not guarantee this.

使用条件When it applies

正则情形 p>0,w>0、q 实，配实分离自伴边界；奇异端点需额外有限性/可积性条件。

In the regular case, p>0,w>0, q is real, and separated real self-adjoint boundary conditions are imposed. Singular endpoints need additional finiteness or integrability conditions.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

Multiply the equation for ym by yn― and subtract the conjugate equation for yn multiplied by ym. Integrating gives (λm−λn―)∫wymyn―=−[p(ym′yn―−ymyn′―)]ab. Self-adjoint boundaries make the right side zero. Taking m=n proves real eigenvalues. Taking distinct eigenvalues proves weighted orthogonality.
