拉梅系数与曲线坐标算符Lamé scale factors and curvilinear differential operators

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

球坐标、柱坐标的一个坐标增量，并不都代表同样的真实长度。先写距离元中的比例因子，再建立梯度和拉普拉斯算符。

Coordinate increments in spherical or cylindrical coordinates do not all represent equal physical lengths. Find the scale factors from the line element before constructing differential operators.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

正交坐标的微小实际长度是 dsi=hidqi。梯度、散度与拉普拉斯算符中的尺度因子，分别来自长度、面积和体积的变化。

Changing a coordinate does not always move you by the same physical distance. In orthogonal coordinates, the actual small length is dsi=hidqi. These scale factors enter the gradient, divergence, and Laplacian because lengths, areas, and volumes change with position.

∇2u=1h1h2h3∑i=13∂∂qi(h1h2h3hi2∂u∂qi)
使用条件When it applies

坐标正交且取右手方向；hi=|∂r/∂qi|，各分量指单位基矢上的物理分量。坐标奇点处用极限或正则性处理。

The coordinates are orthogonal and right-handed. hi=|∂r/∂qi|, and vector components are physical components along unit basis vectors. Handle coordinate singularities by limits or regularity conditions.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

由 du=∑uqidqi=∇u·∑hieidqi 得梯度分量 uqi/hi。对小曲面盒应用高斯公式，i 方向两面的净流量为 ∂qi(Aihjhk)dq1dq2dq3，除以体积 h1h2h3dq1dq2dq3 得散度。令 A=∇u 即得所列拉普拉斯算符。

Write du=∑uqidqi=∇u·∑hieidqi. Matching coefficients gives gradient component uqi/hi. Next apply Gauss's theorem to a tiny coordinate box. The net flux through the two faces perpendicular to direction i is ∂qi(Aihjhk)dq1dq2dq3. Dividing by the volume h1h2h3dq1dq2dq3 gives the divergence. Finally set A=∇u to obtain the stated Laplacian.

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

由拉梅系数推出柱坐标的拉普拉斯算符。

Derive the cylindrical-coordinate Laplacian from the scale factors.

解答

Solution

柱坐标 (ρ,ϕ,z) 的 hρ=1,hϕ=ρ,hz=1，代入得 ∇2u=ρ−1(ρuρ)ρ+ρ−2uϕϕ+uzz。

For cylindrical coordinates (ρ,ϕ,z), hρ=1,hϕ=ρ,hz=1. Substitution gives ∇2u=ρ−1(ρuρ)ρ+ρ−2uϕϕ+uzz.

球谐展开与球内、球外径向解Spherical-harmonic expansions and interior/exterior radial solutions

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

球形边界适合用球谐函数拆分。球内先排除中心发散的项，球外先排除无穷远不符合条件的项，再匹配边界。

Spherical boundaries suit spherical harmonics. Inside a sphere remove terms singular at the center; outside apply the condition at infinity, then match boundary coefficients.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

球面边界最适合球谐函数。轴对称时角函数为 Pl(cosθ)；每一角模态只有两个径向幂 rl 与 r−l−1。球内舍去奇异幂，球外衰减解舍去增长幂。

A spherical boundary suggests spherical harmonics: functions that separate the angular dependence into simple modes. With axial symmetry, the angular functions are Pl(cosθ). Every angular mode has two radial powers, rl and r−l−1. Inside a ball, remove the singular power; outside, remove the growing power when decay is required.

u=∑l=0∞(Alrl+Blr−l−1)Pl(cosθ)
使用条件When it applies

无源区域满足 ∇2u=0；上述形式适用于轴对称。非轴对称需 Ylm(θ,ϕ)；外域须说明无穷远条件。

The source-free region satisfies ∇2u=0. The displayed expansion assumes axial symmetry. Without it, use Ylm(θ,ϕ). An exterior problem needs a condition at infinity.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

令 u=R(r)Θ(θ)，分离常数取 l(l+1)。角方程在 x=cosθ 下为 Legendre 方程；两极有限性选 l=0,1,… 及 Pl。径向方程 (r2R′)′−l(l+1)R=0，试 R=rs 得 s(s+1)=l(l+1)，所以 s=l,−l−1。边界函数系数 al=(2l+1)∫−11f(x)Pl(x)dx/2 来自正交性。

Set u=R(r)Θ(θ) and choose separation constant l(l+1). With x=cosθ, the angular equation becomes Legendre's equation. Finiteness at both poles selects l=0,1,… and Pl. The radial equation is (r2R′)′−l(l+1)R=0. Trying R=rs gives s(s+1)=l(l+1), hence s=l,−l−1. Orthogonality determines the boundary coefficients as al=(2l+1)∫−11f(x)Pl(x)dx/2.

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

半径 R 的球面温度为 2+3cosθ，求球内稳态温度。

A sphere of radius R has surface temperature 2+3cosθ. Find the steady interior temperature.

解答

Solution

边界仅有 P0 和 P1。球心有限给 u=2+3(r/R)cosθ，代入 r=R 即满足边界，两个项均为调和函数。

The boundary contains only P0 and P1. Finiteness at the center gives u=2+3(r/R)cosθ. Substituting r=R recovers the boundary value, and both terms are harmonic.

记忆线索Memory cue

First split the angular data into P or Y modes. Then use the region to select the allowed radial power.

圆柱中的 Bessel 展开Bessel expansions in a cylinder

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

圆柱或圆盘的径向方程会出现Bessel函数。先由中心是否包含在区域内决定保留哪种解，再让外边界挑出允许的零点。

Radial equations in disks and cylinders lead to Bessel functions. Check whether the center belongs to the region, remove inadmissible solutions, and use the outer boundary to select zeros.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

圆柱轴对称径向算符是 R′′+R′/ρ，本征函数为 J0(kρ)，其正交权为 ρ。侧面零温时 k 由 J0(ka)=0 决定；把上下底面先做正弦分解时，径向反而出现 I0。

For axial symmetry, the radial operator is R′′+R′/ρ. Its eigenfunctions are J0(kρ), with orthogonality weight ρ: coefficient integrals must include that extra factor. A zero side boundary selects k through J0(ka)=0. If you first use sine modes to satisfy the two end faces, the radial function becomes I0 instead.

∫0aρJ0(j0nρ/a)J0(j0mρ/a)dρ=a22J1(j0n)2δnm
使用条件When it applies

圆柱包含轴线，排除 Y0 的轴线发散；j0n 为 J0 的第 n 个正零点。Robin 侧面要改本征方程。

The cylinder includes its axis, so exclude Y0, which diverges there. j0n is the nth positive zero of J0. A Robin side boundary changes the eigenvalue equation.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

径向方程可写为 (ρR′)′+k2ρR=0。两不同本征解交叉相乘相减并积分，得 (kn2−km2)∫0aρRnRmdρ=[ρ(RnRm′−RmRn′)]0a，齐次同类边界令右端为零，因此带权正交。范数由 Bessel 递推积分得 a2[J0(ka)2+J1(ka)2]/2，在 J0(ka)=0 时化为所列式。

Write the radial equation as (ρR′)′+k2ρR=0. Multiply two equations for different eigenfunctions by the opposite function, subtract, and integrate: (kn2−km2)∫0aρRnRmdρ=[ρ(RnRm′−RmRn′)]0a. The same homogeneous boundary conditions make the right side zero, proving weighted orthogonality. Bessel recurrence identities give the squared norm a2[J0(ka)2+J1(ka)2]/2. When J0(ka)=0, this reduces to the displayed formula.

如何把常数 1 展开成零侧边界的圆柱径向本征函数？

How do we expand the constant 1 in the radial eigenfunctions for a zero side boundary?

解答

Solution

1=∑n≥1cnJ0(j0nρ/a)，cn=2a2J1(j)2∫0aρJ0(jρ/a)dρ=2/[jJ1(j)]。在开区间成立；端点值由级数边界条件决定。

Write 1=∑n≥1cnJ0(j0nρ/a), with cn=2a2J1(j)2∫0aρJ0(jρ/a)dρ=2/[jJ1(j)]. The expansion holds in the open interval; the series boundary condition determines the endpoint value.

记忆线索Memory cue

Cylindrical projection integrals carry ρ. Satisfy the side first: often J. Satisfy the end faces first: often I.

空间本征值决定扩散速度与振动频率Spatial eigenvalues determine diffusion rates and vibration frequencies

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

空间边界先选出本征值，时间变化随后由它决定。热问题看衰减率，振动问题看频率；两者可以使用同一组空间模态。

Spatial boundaries select eigenvalues, which then set the time behavior. Heat modes decay, while wave modes oscillate; both can use the same spatial eigenfunctions.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

热方程和波动方程分离变量后，共用 ∇2X+k2X=0 的空间本征问题。热问题各模态按 e−Dk2t 衰减；波动问题各模态以 ck 振荡。球中角阶 l 的径向函数是球 Bessel 函数 jl(kr)。

After separation of variables, heat and wave equations share the spatial eigenproblem ∇2X+k2X=0. An eigenfunction is one spatial pattern that evolves without changing shape. A heat mode decays as e−Dk2t; a wave mode oscillates with angular frequency ck. In a sphere, angular order l uses the spherical Bessel function jl(kr).

uheat=∑ncnXne−Dkn2t,uwave=∑nXn[Ancos(cknt)+Bnsin(cknt)]
使用条件When it applies

先把非齐次边界平移成齐次，空间本征算符需配合同类边界；系数按几何体积权重投影。D 为热扩散率，c 为波速。

First subtract a steady solution to make nonzero boundary data homogeneous. Use matching boundary conditions for the spatial eigenproblem and project with the geometric volume weight. D is thermal diffusivity and c is wave speed.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

设 u=XT，ut=D∇2u 给 T′/(DT)=∇2X/X=−k2，故 T=e−Dk2t。波动方程给 T′′+c2k2T=0。球坐标角分离出 l(l+1) 后径向方程为 R′′+2R′/r+[k2−l(l+1)/r2]R=0，有限解为 jl(kr)。

Set u=XT. The heat equation ut=D∇2u gives T′/(DT)=∇2X/X=−k2, so T=e−Dk2t. The wave equation gives T′′+c2k2T=0. In spherical coordinates, separating the angular part introduces l(l+1) and leaves R′′+2R′/r+[k2−l(l+1)/r2]R=0. The finite radial solution is jl(kr).

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

半径 R 的球、球面零温，球对称模态最慢衰减的时间尺度是多少？

For a spherically symmetric temperature in a ball of radius R with zero surface temperature, what is the slowest decay time?

解答

Solution

l=0，j0(kR)=sin(kR)/(kR)=0 给 k1=π/R。最慢指数为 e−Dπ2t/R2，时间尺度 τ=R2/(Dπ2)。

Here l=0. The boundary equation j0(kR)=sin(kR)/(kR)=0 gives k1=π/R. The slowest exponential is e−Dπ2t/R2, so the time scale is τ=R2/(Dπ2).

记忆线索Memory cue

同一空间模态：扩散看 k²，振动看 k。

For the same spatial mode, diffusion uses k² and vibration uses k.

变截面细杆与环形膜Tapered rods and annular membranes

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

杆的截面积改变，或膜的中心挖空，都会改变方程或边界。先重新写物理方程和区域，再判断能否沿用熟悉的函数。

A varying rod area or a hole in a membrane changes the equation or boundary conditions. Write the actual equation and domain before reusing a familiar solution form.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

几何形状会改变微分算符：截面积 A(x) 的细杆纵振动满足 utt=c2A−1(Aux)x；楔杆 A∝x 对应普通 Bessel，锥杆 A∝x2 对应球 Bessel。环形膜不包含原点，不能因 Ym 在零点发散就舍掉它。

Geometry changes the differential operator. A rod with cross-sectional area A(x) obeys utt=c2A−1(Aux)x for longitudinal vibration. A wedge with A∝x leads to ordinary Bessel functions; a cone with A∝x2 leads to spherical Bessel functions. An annular membrane excludes the origin, so divergence of Ym at zero is not a reason to discard it.

(AX′)′+k2AX=0,ω=ck
使用条件When it applies

杆均匀材质、线弹性、小纵振动、细杆近似，c=E/ϱ。膜张力 T、面密度 σ 恒定时 c=√(T/σ)。

The rod has uniform material, linear elasticity, small longitudinal motion, and a slender-rod approximation; c=E/ϱ. For a membrane with constant tension T and surface density σ, c=√(T/σ).

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

取长 dx 的杆元，纵向弹性力为 EAux，两端净力 E(Aux)xdx；质量为 ϱAdx，牛顿定律给出所列方程。代入 u=XT 后得自伴本征方程，因此正交权是 A；自由端条件是 AX′=0，在 A 非零端等价于 X′=0。

Take a rod element of length dx. The longitudinal elastic force is EAux, so the net end force is E(Aux)xdx. Its mass is ϱAdx; Newton's law gives the stated equation. Substitute u=XT to obtain a self-adjoint eigenproblem, whose orthogonality weight is A. A free end has condition AX′=0; where A is nonzero, this is equivalent to X′=0.

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

一端尖、截面积正比于 x 的自由楔杆，其正频率由哪类零点给出？

For a free wedge rod whose area is proportional to x and whose tip is at x=0, which zeros determine the positive frequencies?

解答

Solution

有限径向解 X=J0(kx)，粗端 x=l 自由给 X′(l)=−kJ1(kl)=0，故正频率 ωn=cj1n/l，另有零频刚体平移模态。

The finite radial solution is X=J0(kx). At the thick free end x=l, X′(l)=−kJ1(kl)=0, giving positive frequencies ωn=cj1n/l. There is also a zero-frequency rigid translation mode.

记忆线索Memory cue

先算截面积随 x 的幂，再决定 Bessel 类型；环域保留两类解。

First find how cross-sectional area depends on x, then choose the Bessel type. In a ring domain, keep both independent radial solutions.

A taut annular membrane has fixed inner and outer edges of radii ρ1,ρ2. Find its normal modes and natural frequencies.

用内外两圈固定条件求出允许频率，理解环形膜与圆膜的区别。

Use the two fixed circles to find allowed frequencies and see how an annular membrane differs from a disk.
