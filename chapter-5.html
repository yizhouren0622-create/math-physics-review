拉梅系数与曲线坐标算符Lamé scale factors and curvilinear differential operators

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

球坐标、柱坐标的一个坐标增量，并不都代表同样的真实长度。先写距离元中的比例因子，再建立梯度和拉普拉斯算符。

Coordinate increments in spherical or cylindrical coordinates do not all represent equal physical lengths. Find the scale factors from the line element before constructing differential operators.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

正交坐标的微小实际长度是 dsi=hidqi。梯度、散度与拉普拉斯算符中的尺度因子，分别来自长度、面积和体积的变化。

Changing a coordinate does not always move you by the same physical distance. In orthogonal coordinates, the actual small length is dsi=hidqi. These scale factors enter the gradient, divergence, and Laplacian because lengths, areas, and volumes change with position.

∇2u=1h1h2h3∑i=13∂∂qi(h1h2h3hi2∂u∂qi)
使用条件When it applies

坐标正交且取右手方向；hi=|∂r/∂qi|，各分量指单位基矢上的物理分量。坐标奇点处用极限或正则性处理。

The coordinates are orthogonal and right-handed. hi=|∂r/∂qi|, and vector components are physical components along unit basis vectors. Handle coordinate singularities by limits or regularity conditions.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

先从“函数改变量”求梯度。正交坐标中的位移为 dr=∑ihieidqi，而 du=∑iuqidqi=∇u·dr。比较每个 dqi 的系数，得到 (∇u)i=uqi/hi；除以 hi 是把每单位坐标变化换成每单位实际长度的变化。

Start with the change in a scalar function to find its gradient. In orthogonal coordinates, dr=∑ihieidqi, while du=∑iuqidqi=∇u·dr. Comparing the coefficient of each dqi gives (∇u)i=uqi/hi. Division by hi converts change per coordinate increment into change per physical length.

再看一个小坐标盒。垂直于第 i 个方向的面，其面积为 hjhkdqjdqk，其中 i,j,k 互不相同。相对两面的外向通量相减，一阶项为 ∂qi(Aihjhk)dq1dq2dq3：面面积随位置的变化也必须放进导数。

Next consider a small coordinate box. A face normal to direction i has area hjhkdqjdqk, where i,j,k are distinct. Subtracting the outward fluxes through the opposite faces gives the first-order term ∂qi(Aihjhk)dq1dq2dq3. The variation of face area with position must be included inside the derivative.

三对面通量相加，再除盒子体积 h1h2h3dq1dq2dq3，小盒极限给 ∇·A=(h1h2h3)−1∑i∂qi(Aihjhk)。这就是“单位体积净流出量”；用 hjhk=h1h2h3/hi 可统一三个方向。

Add the three face-pair fluxes and divide by the volume h1h2h3dq1dq2dq3. Shrinking the box gives ∇·A=(h1h2h3)−1∑i∂qi(Aihjhk). This is net outflow per unit volume. The relation hjhk=h1h2h3/hi treats the three directions uniformly.

拉普拉斯就是梯度再取散度。把 Ai=uqi/hi 放进上一式，得到 ∇2u=(h1h2h3)−1∑i∂qi[(h1h2h3/hi2)uqi]。这套推导针对正交坐标；在轴线或极点等坐标奇点，应先在普通点计算，再用有限性和连续极限解释。

The Laplacian is the divergence of the gradient. Insert Ai=uqi/hi into the preceding expression to get ∇2u=(h1h2h3)−1∑i∂qi[(h1h2h3/hi2)uqi]. This derivation assumes orthogonal coordinates. At coordinate singularities such as an axis or pole, calculate at regular points first and interpret the result through finiteness and continuous limits.

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

由拉梅系数推出柱坐标的拉普拉斯算符。

Derive the cylindrical-coordinate Laplacian from the scale factors.

解答

Solution

柱坐标 (ρ,ϕ,z) 中，沿半径和高度的小长度就是 dρ,dz，沿角度的小弧长是 ρdϕ，所以 hρ=1,hϕ=ρ,hz=1，乘积为 ρ。

In cylindrical coordinates (ρ,ϕ,z), small radial and vertical lengths are dρ,dz, while an angular increment has arc length ρdϕ. Thus hρ=1,hϕ=ρ,hz=1, with product ρ.

三个导数括号中的系数依次为 ρ,1/ρ,ρ。逐项写成 ∇2u=ρ−1[∂ρ(ρuρ)+∂ϕ(ρ−1uϕ)+∂z(ρuz)]，再用 ρ 与 ϕ,z 无关，得到 ρ−1(ρuρ)ρ+ρ−2uϕϕ+uzz。

The coefficients inside the three derivative brackets are ρ,1/ρ,ρ. Write ∇2u=ρ−1[∂ρ(ρuρ)+∂ϕ(ρ−1uϕ)+∂z(ρuz)]. Since ρ is independent of ϕ,z, this becomes ρ−1(ρuρ)ρ+ρ−2uϕϕ+uzz.

自检取 u=ρ2=x2+y2：径向项给 ρ−1∂ρ(2ρ2)=4，另两项为零，与直角坐标 ∂x2x2+∂y2y2=4 一致。

Check with u=ρ2=x2+y2. The radial term gives ρ−1∂ρ(2ρ2)=4, and the other terms vanish, matching the Cartesian value ∂x2x2+∂y2y2=4.

柱坐标为 (ρ,ϕ,z)，函数 u=ρ2z 与角度无关。在 ρ>0 求 Δu，再判断结果能否连续延伸到轴线。可使用柱坐标尺度因子 hρ=1,hϕ=ρ,hz=1。

In cylindrical coordinates (ρ,ϕ,z), let u=ρ2z be independent of the angle. Find Δu for ρ>0, and decide whether the result extends continuously to the axis. You may use the scale factors hρ=1,hϕ=ρ,hz=1.

提示 1：先选方法Hint 1: choose a method＋

先用曲线坐标的拉普拉斯公式，不要把 ρ 当成直角坐标。

Use the curvilinear Laplacian first; do not treat ρ as a Cartesian coordinate.

提示 2：写第一行Hint 2: write the first line＋

无角度依赖时，第一行是 Δu=ρ−1(ρuρ)ρ+uzz。

With no angular dependence, the first line is Δu=ρ−1(ρuρ)ρ+uzz.

提示 3：关键计算Hint 3: key calculation＋

先算 uρ=2ρz，于是 (ρuρ)ρ=4ρz；另外 uzz=0。

First find uρ=2ρz, hence (ρuρ)ρ=4ρz; also uzz=0.

展开完整解答Open the full solution＋

沿角度方向的实际长度是 ρdϕ，所以径向项要保留面积变化带来的因子。由给定尺度因子，Δu=ρ−1(ρuρ)ρ+ρ−2uϕϕ+uzz。

The physical length in the angular direction is ρdϕ, so the radial term must retain the factor caused by the changing area. The given scale factors yield Δu=ρ−1(ρuρ)ρ+ρ−2uϕϕ+uzz.

本题与角度无关，故 uϕϕ=0。径向先求一次导数：uρ=2ρz；再乘以半径：ρuρ=2ρ2z；最后求导：(ρuρ)ρ=4ρz。

There is no angular dependence, so uϕϕ=0. For the radial part, differentiate once: uρ=2ρz; multiply by the radius: ρuρ=2ρ2z; then differentiate: (ρuρ)ρ=4ρz.

函数关于高度只有一次项，故 uzz=0。合并得 Δu=4z。虽然计算公式出现除以半径，最后结果没有奇异性，能连续延伸到轴线。

The height dependence is linear, so uzz=0. Combining terms gives Δu=4z. Although the coordinate formula divides by the radius, the final result has no singularity and extends continuously to the axis.

怎样检查，哪里容易错Check your result and common mistakes＋

改写为 u=(x2+y2)z，有 uxx=2z,uyy=2z,uzz=0，相加仍为 4z。典型错误是只算 uρρ=2z，漏掉 uρ/ρ=2z。

Write u=(x2+y2)z in Cartesian coordinates. Then uxx=2z,uyy=2z,uzz=0, whose sum is again 4z. A common error is to calculate only uρρ=2z, omitting uρ/ρ=2z.

球谐展开与球内、球外径向解Spherical-harmonic expansions and interior/exterior radial solutions

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

球形边界适合用球谐函数拆分。球内先排除中心发散的项，球外先排除无穷远不符合条件的项，再匹配边界。

Spherical boundaries suit spherical harmonics. Inside a sphere remove terms singular at the center; outside apply the condition at infinity, then match boundary coefficients.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

球面边界最适合球谐函数。轴对称时角函数为 Pl(cosθ)；每一角模态只有两个径向幂 rl 与 r−l−1。球内舍去奇异幂，球外衰减解舍去增长幂。

A spherical boundary suggests spherical harmonics: functions that separate the angular dependence into simple modes. With axial symmetry, the angular functions are Pl(cosθ). Every angular mode has two radial powers, rl and r−l−1. Inside a ball, remove the singular power; outside, remove the growing power when decay is required.

u=∑l=0∞(Alrl+Blr−l−1)Pl(cosθ)
使用条件When it applies

无源区域满足 ∇2u=0；上述形式适用于轴对称。非轴对称需 Ylm(θ,ϕ)；外域须说明无穷远条件。

The source-free region satisfies ∇2u=0. The displayed expansion assumes axial symmetry. Without it, use Ylm(θ,ϕ). An exterior problem needs a condition at infinity.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

核心推导先限于轴对称、无源区域。令 u=R(r)Θ(θ)，代入球坐标 Laplace 方程并乘 r2/(RΘ)，得 (r2R′)′/R+[(sinθΘ′)′/(sinθΘ)]=0。一项只依赖 r、另一项只依赖 θ，所以分别为常数 λ 和 −λ。

For the core derivation, assume an axisymmetric source-free region. Set u=R(r)Θ(θ) in the spherical Laplace equation and multiply by r2/(RΘ). This gives (r2R′)′/R+[(sinθΘ′)′/(sinθΘ)]=0. One term depends only on r and the other only on θ, so they equal constants λ and −λ.

令 x=cosθ，角方程化为 [(1−x2)Θx]x+λΘ=0。采用 Legendre 方程的正则本征结果：在两极有限的角模态取 λ=l(l+1)、Θ=Pl(x)，l=0,1,…。这一步调用已知本征结果，不是仅靠形式分离便证明了所有函数的展开完备性。

With x=cosθ, the angular equation becomes [(1−x2)Θx]x+λΘ=0. Use the regular Legendre eigenvalue result: angular modes finite at both poles have λ=l(l+1) and Θ=Pl(x), for l=0,1,…. This invokes a known eigenvalue result; formal separation alone does not prove completeness for every boundary function.

径向方程为 (r2R′)′−l(l+1)R=0。试 R=rs，得 s(s+1)=l(l+1)，因式分解为 (s−l)(s+l+1)=0，故每阶有 rl,r−l−1 两种径向解。球内有限性删去负幂；球外若要求趋零，删去增长幂及常数阶的非零常数；球壳则通常两种都留。

The radial equation is (r2R′)′−l(l+1)R=0. Try R=rs to obtain s(s+1)=l(l+1), or (s−l)(s+l+1)=0. Each order therefore has radial solutions rl,r−l−1. Finiteness inside a ball removes the negative power. Exterior decay removes growing powers and the nonzero constant at order zero. In a shell, both powers generally remain.

最后用边界确定幅值。若球面数据为 f(x)=∑lalPl(x)，乘 Pm 并在 [−1,1] 积分，正交式 ∫−11PlPmdx=2δlm/(2l+1) 只保留第 m 项，因此 al=(2l+1)∫−11f(x)Pl(x)dx/2。非轴对称数据改用 Ylm，不能只保留 Pl。

Finally determine amplitudes from the boundary. If f(x)=∑lalPl(x), multiply by Pm and integrate over [−1,1]. Orthogonality, ∫−11PlPmdx=2δlm/(2l+1), leaves only order m, giving al=(2l+1)∫−11f(x)Pl(x)dx/2. Non-axisymmetric data requires Ylm, rather than only Pl.

做一道简单例题Try a worked example＋

半径 R 的球面温度为 2+3cosθ，求球内稳态温度。

A sphere of radius R has surface temperature 2+3cosθ. Find the steady interior temperature.

解答

Solution

先认出 P0=1,P1(cosθ)=cosθ，所以表面 f=2P0+3P1，只有 a0=2,a1=3 非零。

Identify P0=1,P1(cosθ)=cosθ. The surface data is f=2P0+3P1, so only a0=2,a1=3 are nonzero.

球内舍去 r−l−1，并让每个径向因子在表面等于 1：第零阶用 1，第一阶用 r/R。于是 u=2+3(r/R)cosθ。

Discard r−l−1 inside the ball and normalize each radial factor to 1 on the surface. Order zero uses 1, and order one uses r/R. Thus u=2+3(r/R)cosθ.

代 r=R 得 2+3cosθ，代 r=0 得有限值 2。还可写 u=2+3z/R，其二阶偏导全部为零，因此确实调和。

At r=R this gives 2+3cosθ, and at r=0 it gives the finite value 2. Equivalently, u=2+3z/R has zero second derivatives, confirming harmonicity.

半径为 2 的球外没有源。求轴对称调和函数 u(r,θ)，满足 r>2、u(2,θ)=3cosθ 以及 u→0 当 r→∞。可用 P1(cosθ)=cosθ，第一阶径向解为 r 和 r−2。

There are no sources outside a sphere of radius 2. Find the axisymmetric harmonic function u(r,θ) for r>2 with u(2,θ)=3cosθ and u→0 as r→∞. You may use P1(cosθ)=cosθ; the first-order radial solutions are r and r−2.

提示 1：先选方法Hint 1: choose a method＋

边界只有 P1 模态；球外条件决定保留哪种径向幂。

The boundary contains only the P1 mode. The exterior condition selects the radial power.

提示 2：写第一行Hint 2: write the first line＋

衰减条件删去增长项，因此先写 u=Br−2cosθ。

The decay condition removes the growing term, so start with u=Br−2cosθ.

提示 3：关键计算Hint 3: key calculation＋

At the sphere, B/4=3, hence B=12.

展开完整解答Open the full solution＋

先识别角度：3cosθ=3P1(cosθ)，所以只需第一阶角模态，不必求无限多个系数。

Identify the angular dependence first: 3cosθ=3P1(cosθ). Only the first angular mode is needed; there is no infinite list of coefficients to compute.

第一阶通解是 u=(Ar+Br−2)cosθ。球外要求趋零，增长项必须消失，因此 A=0。

The first-order general solution is u=(Ar+Br−2)cosθ. Decay in the exterior removes the growing term, so A=0.

把球面半径代入，得 u(2,θ)=(B/4)cosθ。与给定边界比较得 B=12，故 u=12r−2cosθ。

Substitute the sphere radius to obtain u(2,θ)=(B/4)cosθ. Matching the boundary gives B=12, and therefore u=12r−2cosθ.

怎样检查，哪里容易错Check your result and common mistakes＋

代 r=2 恢复 3cosθ；当 r→∞ 时确实趋零。径向方程也满足：(r2R′)′−2R=0，其中 R=12r−2。典型错误是把球内的 r 模态保留下来，导致远场增长。

Setting r=2 recovers 3cosθ, and the solution tends to zero as r→∞. The radial equation is also satisfied: (r2R′)′−2R=0 with R=12r−2. A common error is to retain the interior r mode, which grows in the far field.

圆柱中的 Bessel 展开Bessel expansions in a cylinder

先用一句话理解Begin with the idea

圆柱或圆盘的径向方程会出现Bessel函数。先由中心是否包含在区域内决定保留哪种解，再让外边界挑出允许的零点。

Radial equations in disks and cylinders lead to Bessel functions. Check whether the center belongs to the region, remove inadmissible solutions, and use the outer boundary to select zeros.

再看数学表述Open the mathematical explanation＋

圆柱轴对称径向算符是 R′′+R′/ρ，本征函数为 J0(kρ)，其正交权为 ρ。侧面零温时 k 由 J0(ka)=0 决定；把上下底面先做正弦分解时，径向反而出现 I0。

For axial symmetry, the radial operator is R′′+R′/ρ. Its eigenfunctions are J0(kρ), with orthogonality weight ρ: coefficient integrals must include that extra factor. A zero side boundary selects k through J0(ka)=0. If you first use sine modes to satisfy the two end faces, the radial function becomes I0 instead.

∫0aρJ0(j0nρ/a)J0(j0mρ/a)dρ=a22J1(j0n)2δnm
使用条件When it applies

圆柱包含轴线，排除 Y0 的轴线发散；j0n 为 J0 的第 n 个正零点。Robin 侧面要改本征方程。

The cylinder includes its axis, so exclude Y0, which diverges there. j0n is the nth positive zero of J0. A Robin side boundary changes the eigenvalue equation.

展开证明 / 推导Open proof / derivation＋

侧面先做零值边界时，轴对称径向方程为 R′′+R′/ρ+k2R=0，即 (ρR′)′+k2ρR=0。轴线有限选 Rn=J0(knρ)，侧面 Rn(a)=0 决定 kn=j0n/a。方程里的 ρR 已提示投影的权为 ρ。

When a zero side value is satisfied first, the axisymmetric radial equation is R′′+R′/ρ+k2R=0, or (ρR′)′+k2ρR=0. Axis finiteness selects Rn=J0(knρ), and Rn(a)=0 gives kn=j0n/a. The factor ρR already identifies the projection weight ρ.

取两个不同根，把第 n 个方程乘 Rm、第 m 个乘 Rn，相减后积分。乘积求导合并成边界项，得到 (kn2−km2)∫0aρRnRmdρ=[ρ(RnRm′−RmRn′)]0a。

Take two different roots, multiply equation n by Rm and equation m by Rn, subtract, and integrate. The product rule combines derivative terms into the boundary expression (kn2−km2)∫0aρRnRmdρ=[ρ(RnRm′−RmRn′)]0a.

在 ρ=a，两个函数都为零；在 ρ=0，有限的 J0 及其导数使带 ρ 的边界项趋零。因此不同根的积分为零。相同的齐次 Robin 条件也能让边界项相消，但它会改变允许的根，不能仍用 J0 的零点。

At ρ=a, both functions vanish. At ρ=0, finite J0 functions and derivatives make the boundary term with its factor ρ vanish. Thus different roots have zero weighted inner product. A common homogeneous Robin condition also cancels the boundary term, but changes the allowed roots; the zeros of J0 can no longer be reused.
