第 1 题Question 110 分 · 调和共轭10 points · Harmonic conjugate

从实部恢复函数Recover a function from its real part

已知解析函数实部为 excosy 且 f(0)=1+2i，求 f(z)。

An analytic function has real part excosy and satisfies f(0)=1+2i. Find f(z).

展开提示Open hint＋

识别 ez，或按 C–R 求虚部。

Recognize ez, or integrate the Cauchy–Riemann equations.

展开答案与评分要点Open solution and marking guide＋

vy=ux=excosy，故 v=exsiny+C(x)；再由 vx=−uy=exsiny 得 C′=0。于是 f=ez+iC，初值给 C=2，故 f=ez+2i。

From vy=ux=excosy we get v=exsiny+C(x). Then vx=−uy=exsiny implies C′=0. Thus f=ez+iC; the specified value gives C=2, so f=ez+2i.

评分要点

Marking guide

C–R及积分4分；确定常数3分；完整函数3分。

Cauchy–Riemann integration: 4 points; constant: 3 points; analytic function: 3 points.

第 2 题Question 210 分 · 奇点分类10 points · Singularity classification

零点抵消与留数Cancellation of zeros and a residue

判断 f(z)=(ez−1−z)/z3 在原点的奇点类型与留数。

Classify the singularity of f(z)=(ez−1−z)/z3 at the origin and find its residue.

展开提示Open hint＋

展开分子到三次项；不要只看分母三次幂。

Expand the numerator through the cubic term. The denominator alone does not determine the pole order.

展开答案与评分要点Open solution and marking guide＋

ez−1−z=z2/2+z3/6+⋯，故 f=1/(2z)+1/6+z/24+⋯。原点为一阶极点，留数为 1/2。

We have ez−1−z=z2/2+z3/6+⋯, hence f=1/(2z)+1/6+z/24+⋯. The origin is a simple pole and its residue is 1/2.

评分要点

Marking guide

分子展开4分；极点阶数3分；留数3分。

Series: 4 points; pole order: 3 points; residue: 3 points.

第 3 题Question 310 分 · 平移性质10 points · Translation rule

带平移的傅里叶变换A shifted Fourier transform

已知 F[e−a|x|]=2a/(a2+k2)，求 e−a|x−b| 的变换，采用核 e−ikx。

Given F[e−a|x|]=2a/(a2+k2), find the transform of e−a|x−b| using kernel e−ikx.

展开提示Open hint＋

换元 t=x−b，指数多出相位。

Substitute t=x−b and track the resulting phase factor.

展开答案与评分要点Open solution and marking guide＋

x=t+b，所以 e−ikx=e−ikbe−ikt。因此 F(k)=e−ikb2a/(a2+k2)。平移不改变变换模长，只改变相位；相位负号由核的约定决定。

We have x=t+b, so e−ikx=e−ikbe−ikt. Thus F(k)=e−ikb2a/(a2+k2). Translation preserves the transform’s magnitude and changes its phase. The phase sign follows from the chosen kernel.

评分要点

Marking guide

换元3分；相位符号3分；完整式与解释4分。

Substitution: 3 points; phase sign: 3 points; result and interpretation: 4 points.

第 4 题Question 410 分 · 单位阶跃10 points · Heaviside step

Laplace 延迟与初值Laplace delay and initial data

求 y′+y=H(t−2)，y(0)=0。

Solve y′+y=H(t−2), y(0)=0.

展开提示Open hint＋

右侧变换 e−2s/s；逆变换用延迟性质。

The right-hand side transforms to e−2s/s. Use the time-delay rule when inverting.

展开答案与评分要点Open solution and marking guide＋

(s+1)Y=e−2s/s，所以 Y=e−2s[1/s−1/(s+1)]。逆变换为 y=H(t−2)[1−e−(t−2)]。t<2 为零，t>2 满足受迫方程；在 t=2 解连续，因此一阶导数没有额外δ项。

We have (s+1)Y=e−2s/s, hence Y=e−2s[1/s−1/(s+1)]. The inverse transform is y=H(t−2)[1−e−(t−2)]. For t<2 the solution is zero; for t>2 it satisfies the forced equation. At t=2 the solution is continuous, so its first derivative contains no additional delta term.

评分要点

Marking guide

变换4分；拆分与延迟4分；连续性核验2分。

Transformed equation: 4 points; partial fractions and delay: 4 points; continuity: 2 points.

第 5 题Question 510 分 · 行波10 points · Traveling waves

无限弦的达朗贝解D’Alembert’s solution on an infinite string

无限弦 utt=c2uxx，初位移 ϕ(x)=e−x2，初速度零。求解并解释传播。

On an infinite string utt=c2uxx, with initial displacement ϕ(x)=e−x2 and zero initial velocity, find and interpret the solution.

展开提示Open hint＋

初速度零时两列行波各承担一半初位移。

Split the initial displacement into two traveling waves, each with half the amplitude.

展开答案与评分要点Open solution and marking guide＋

达朗贝公式 u=[ϕ(x−ct)+ϕ(x+ct)]/2+(1/(2c))∫x−ctx+ctψ(s)ds，此处 ψ=0，故 u=12[e−(x−ct)2+e−(x+ct)2]。第一项向右以速率 c 传播，第二项向左；代入 t=0 恢复初位移，时间导数相消。

D’Alembert’s formula is u=[ϕ(x−ct)+ϕ(x+ct)]/2+(1/(2c))∫x−ctx+ctψ(s)ds. Here ψ=0, giving u=12[e−(x−ct)2+e−(x+ct)2]. The first term travels right at speed c, and the second travels left. Setting t=0 recovers the displacement; their time derivatives cancel initially.

评分要点

Marking guide

公式4分；代入3分；方向与初值3分。

Formula: 4 points; substitution: 3 points; directions and initial conditions: 3 points.

第 6 题Question 610 分 · Neumann 边界10 points · Neumann boundary conditions

热方程中的常数模态The constant heat mode

杆 0<x<L 满足 ut=κuxx，两端绝热，初温 u(x,0)=T0+Acos(2πx/L)。求解并给出长时间极限。

A rod 0<x<L obeys ut=κuxx, is insulated at both ends, and starts from u(x,0)=T0+Acos(2πx/L). Find its temperature and long-time limit.

展开提示Open hint＋

绝热边界用余弦；常数模态本征值为零。

Use cosine modes. The constant mode has eigenvalue zero.

展开答案与评分要点Open solution and marking guide＋

cos(2πx/L) 的空间本征值为 (2π/L)2，常数模态不衰减。因此 u=T0+Ae−4κπ2t/L2cos(2πx/L)。两端 ux=0。当 t→∞，u→T0；这就是守恒的平均温度。

The spatial eigenvalues for cos(2πx/L) are (2π/L)2. The constant mode does not decay. Thus u=T0+Ae−4κπ2t/L2cos(2πx/L). At both ends ux=0. As t→∞, we have u→T0: this is the conserved mean temperature.

评分要点

Marking guide

基底与零模态3分；解4分；长时极限与物理意义3分。

Basis and zero mode: 3 points; solution: 4 points; limit and meaning: 3 points.

第 7 题Question 710 分 · Wronskian10 points · Wronskian

Abel 公式与条件Abel’s formula and its conditions

对 y′′+2y′+y=0，已知 y1=e−x,y2=xe−x。计算 W=y1y2′−y1′y2，并用 Abel 公式核验。

For y′′+2y′+y=0 and the given solutions y1=e−x,y2=xe−x, calculate W=y1y2′−y1′y2 and check Abel’s formula.

展开提示Open hint＋

标准形式的 y′ 系数为2，所以 W=Ce−2x。

The coefficient of y′ is 2, so W=Ce−2x.

展开答案与评分要点Open solution and marking guide＋

y1′=−e−x，y2′=(1−x)e−x，故 W=(1−x)e−2x+xe−2x=e−2x。Abel公式 W′=−2W 给 W=Ce−2x；由 W(0)=1 得 C=1。W≠0，两解线性独立。

We have y1′=−e−x and y2′=(1−x)e−x, hence W=(1−x)e−2x+xe−2x=e−2x. Abel’s formula W′=−2W gives W=Ce−2x; using W(0)=1 yields C=1. Since W≠0, the two solutions are linearly independent.

评分要点

Marking guide

求导及行列式4分；Abel核验4分；独立性2分。

Derivatives and Wronskian: 4 points; Abel check: 4 points; independence: 2 points.

第 8 题Question 810 分 · 球函数10 points · Spherical functions

Legendre 正交展开Orthogonal expansion in Legendre polynomials

把 x2 在 [−1,1] 上展开成 Legendre 多项式，并求 ∫−11x2P2(x)dx。

Expand x2 on [−1,1] and evaluate ∫−11x2P2(x)dx.

展开提示Open hint＋

P2=(3x2−1)/2；范数为 2/(2l+1)。

Use P2=(3x2−1)/2 and the norm 2/(2l+1).

展开答案与评分要点Open solution and marking guide＋

由定义直接得 x2=P0/3+2P2/3。乘 P2 积分，正交性消去 P0 项，得到 (2/3)·[2/(2·2+1)]=4/15。也可将 (3x4−x2)/2 直接积分验算。

The definitions give x2=P0/3+2P2/3. Multiply by P2 and integrate. Orthogonality eliminates the P0 term, leaving (2/3)·[2/(2·2+1)]=4/15. Direct integration of (3x4−x2)/2 checks the result.

评分要点

Marking guide

展开4分；正交性与范数3分；积分结果3分。

Expansion: 4 points; orthogonality and norm: 3 points; result: 3 points.

第 9 题Question 910 分 · Bessel 本征值10 points · Bessel eigenfunctions

圆膜的最低径向模态The lowest radial mode of a circular membrane

半径 a 的圆膜满足 utt=c2∇2u，边缘固定，轴对称且中心有界。写出径向本征函数与固有角频率；初形为 AJ0(j0,1r/a)、初速度零时给出振动。

A circular membrane of radius a obeys utt=c2∇2u, has a fixed edge, and is axisymmetric and bounded at the center. Find its radial modes and frequencies. For initial shape AJ0(j0,1r/a) and zero initial velocity, find its motion.

展开提示Open hint＋

中心有界舍去 Y0，边缘零要求 J0(ka)=0。

Discard Y0 at the center. The edge requires J0(ka)=0.

展开答案与评分要点Open solution and marking guide＋

轴对称径向方程为 R′′+R′/r+k2R=0。有界解 J0(kr)；设 j0,n 为 J0 第 n 个正零点，则 Rn=J0(j0,nr/a)，ωn=cj0,n/a。所给初形只激发第一径向模态，u=AJ0(j0,1r/a)cos(cj0,1t/a)。

The axisymmetric radial equation is R′′+R′/r+k2R=0. Its bounded solution is J0(kr). Let j0,n be the n-th positive zero of J0. Then Rn=J0(j0,nr/a) and ωn=cj0,n/a. The given shape excites only the first radial mode, giving u=AJ0(j0,1r/a)cos(cj0,1t/a).

评分要点

Marking guide

径向方程3分；边界定零点与频率4分；单模态解3分。

Radial equation: 3 points; zeros and frequencies: 4 points; single-mode solution: 3 points.

第 10 题Question 1010 分 · 基本解10 points · Fundamental solution

点源与格林函数符号A point source and the Green-function sign

全空间求解 −∇2u=qδ(r−r0)，要求无穷远趋零。写出解，并用小球通量验证常数。

Solve −∇2u=qδ(r−r0) with decay at infinity. Check normalization using the flux through a small sphere.

展开提示Open hint＋

利用 ∇2(1/r)=−4πδ；向外法向导数是负的。

Use ∇2(1/r)=−4πδ. The outward radial derivative is negative.

展开答案与评分要点Open solution and marking guide＋

令 R=|r−r0|，有 u=q/(4πR)。源外调和。围绕源点取半径 ε 小球，∂nu=−q/(4πε2)，故 −∫Sε∂nudS=q，与积分后的源强一致；无穷远趋零。

Let R=|r−r0|. Then u=q/(4πR), harmonic away from the source. On a small sphere of radius ε centered at the source, ∂nu=−q/(4πε2), hence −∫Sε∂nudS=q. This matches the integrated source strength. The solution also decays at infinity.

评分要点

Marking guide

解与常数4分；通量验证4分；域外和无穷远2分。

Solution and constant: 4 points; flux check: 4 points; source-free region and infinity: 2 points.
